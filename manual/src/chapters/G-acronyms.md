---
title: Acronyms and Abbreviations
abstract: The acronyms and abbreviations used in this manual, with their meanings and the chapters in which they are defined or mainly used.
---

Space engineering is notoriously rich in acronyms. This list gives every acronym that is spelled out in the chapters, together with common ones used without definition. The last column gives the chapters where each is defined or mainly used (letters denote appendices). Acronyms in other languages are followed by the English meaning where helpful. Units and chemical formulae are not listed; see Appendix A.
