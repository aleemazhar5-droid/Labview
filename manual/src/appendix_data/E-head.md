This appendix collects reference data that would interrupt the flow of the chapters. The launch statistics and launch logs are generated directly from the **General Catalog of Artificial Space Objects (GCAT)** maintained by Jonathan McDowell (planet4589.org/space/gcat, licensed CC-BY 4.0), as updated on 13 October 2026. An orbital launch attempt is counted when a vehicle intended to reach orbit lifts off, whether or not it succeeds; suborbital test flights (including most Starship flights before September 2026) are not included. Small differences from other published counts arise from the treatment of such tests, of launches from one country by another's vehicle, and of partial failures.
