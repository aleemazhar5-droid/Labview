# Chapter 51 — currency notes (facts checked 1 November 2026 unless stated)

## Astronomy and astrophysics
- AstroNet (Shallue & Vanderburg, AJ 2018; announced 14 Dec 2017): CNN trained on ~15 000 labelled Kepler signals; ~96 % accuracy on test set; run on 670 stars with multiple candidates; found Kepler-90i (8th planet, 14.4-day orbit) and Kepler-80g. https://science.nasa.gov/universe/exoplanets/discovery-of-eight-planets-makes-alien-system-the-first-to-tie-with-our-solar-system ; https://www.theregister.com/2017/12/15/ai_finds_star_systems/
- ExoMiner (Valizadegan et al., ApJ 2022; Nov 2021): 1 922 unlabelled KOIs → 368 with score > 0.99 (46 already validated); 301 new validated planets; recall 93.6 % at 99 % precision vs 76.3 % for best existing classifier. https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.10009
- ExoMiner++ (Valizadegan et al., arXiv Feb 2025, AJ Nov 2025): 147 568 unlabelled 2-min TESS TCEs → 7 330 planet candidates (1 868 existing TOIs, 69 CTOIs, 50 new CTOIs); of 2 506 TOIs labelled PC on ExoFOP, 1 797 kept as PC. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.09790
- Kepler DR25 KOI catalogue (Thompson et al. 2018): 8 054 KOIs, 4 034 planet candidates; Robovetter rule-based vetting; low-SNR 200–500 d candidates 76.7 % complete, 50.5 % reliable (FGK dwarfs). https://ipac.caltech.edu/publication/2018ApJS..235...38T
- NASA Exoplanet Archive (Oct 2026, via ch39 notes): 6 451 confirmed planets; TESS 8 151 TOIs, 1 012 confirmed.
- RV stellar-activity CNN (de Beurs et al., AJ 164, 2022): HARPS-N solar telescope, >600 observations over 3 yr; RV scatter 1.753 → 1.039 m/s. https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.00003
- Rubin Observatory: First Look June 2025; first world-public alerts reached brokers night of 24 Feb 2026 (~800 000 alerts that night, Sky & Telescope); LSST 10-year survey formally begun by 30 Jun 2026; ~10 TB of data per night; up to ~7 million alerts per night, ~20 billion over 10 yr (NOIRLab current figure; design value 10 million). 3 200-Mpixel camera, image every ~40 s; >11 000 new asteroids in ~1.5 months of early optimisation surveys. https://www.washington.edu/news/2026/06/30/rubin-observatory-legacy-survey-space-time-lsst/ ; https://noirlab.edu/science/news/announcements/sci26008 ; https://skyandtelescope.org/uncategorized/rubin-observatory-sends-800000-alerts-to-astronomers-per-night/
- Rubin alert key numbers (DMTN-102, 2024): design 10 000 alerts/visit, ~1 000 visits/night → ~10 M/night; ≤82 KB per alert → ~782 GB/night; 98 % of alerts within 60 s of readout. https://dmtn-102.lsst.io/
- Rubin brokers: seven full-stream (ALeRCE, AMPEL, ANTARES, Babamul, Fink, Lasair, Pitt-Google) plus two downstream services (SNAPS, POI Broker). https://rubinobservatory.org/for-scientists/data-products/alerts-and-brokers
- PLAsTiCC (Kaggle, 28 Sep–17 Dec 2018): >1 000 teams; ~7 850 training objects; winner K. Boone (GP fits + augmentation + boosted trees). https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.12392
- LSST photo-z (Science Book): rms σz/(1+z) < 0.05, goal 0.02; 3σ outliers < 10 %; bias < 0.003, gold sample i < 25.3. https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.0201
- Euclid Q1 strong lenses (A&A 711, A26, 2026; arXiv Mar 2025): 497 galaxy–galaxy lenses in 63 deg²; 250 grade A (243 new); deep-learning ensemble incl. Zoobot → Space Warps volunteers → experts; ~7 000 grade A/B expected in DR1, >100 000 in full Wide Survey. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.15324
- Galaxy Zoo DESI (Walmsley et al., MNRAS 2023): Zoobot morphology for 8.67 million galaxies. Euclid prep XLIII: ~800 million Euclid galaxies measurable. https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2023MNRAS.526.4768W/abstract
- JWST CEERS morphology (Huertas-Company et al., A&A 685, A48, 2024): ~20 000 galaxies, CNN trained on HST labels and domain-adapted; 80–90 % agreement with visual. https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024A&A...685A..48H
- SimBIG field-level SBI (arXiv 2310.15256): BOSS CMASS subsample; σ8 2.65× tighter than standard power-spectrum analysis; Ωm similar. https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15256
- DINGO-BNS (Dax et al., Nature 639, 49, 5 Mar 2025): full inference of BNS parameters in ~1 s; sky localisation ~30 % tighter; pre-merger alerts. https://sciencedaily.com/releases/2025/03/250305134808.htm
- Deep Loop Shaping (Science 389, Sept 2025, DeepMind + LIGO): RL controller on LIGO Livingston; control noise 10–30 Hz reduced >30×, up to 100× in sub-bands; proof of concept. https://www.ligo.caltech.edu/news/ligo20250908
- LVK O4: 24 May 2023 – 18 Nov 2025; ~250 real-time candidates; GWTC-4.0 (Aug 2025) 128 new significant events from O4a, 218 confident detections in total. https://www.ligo.caltech.edu/news/ligo20251118 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.18080
- Aframe: ML low-latency CBC search, research pipeline (MIT/LIGO), tested in O4 MDC. https://arxiv.org/html/2508.14242v1
- FRBs: CNN on Breakthrough Listen GBT C-band data (Sep 2018) found 72 new pulses from FRB 121102 (21 → 93 in 5 h). Second CHIME/FRB Catalog (arXiv Jan 2026): 4 539 bursts from 3 641 sources (981 bursts from 83 repeaters), Jul 2018–Sep 2023. https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.03043 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09399
- SKA: ~700 PB/yr of science data products (estimate); SKA-Low first image 17 Mar 2025. https://www.csiro.au/en/news/All/News/2025/March/SKA-Low-first-image

## Planetary science
- Lunar rockfalls (Bickel et al., Nat. Commun. 2020): 136 610 rockfalls, 80°N–80°S, CNN on LRO NAC images. https://b2find.eudat.eu/dataset/3ccf0070-f75d-50ab-91b9-4a2b8a7eb6bd
- Lunar boulders (Aussel et al., JGR Planets 130, Jul 2025): ~94 million boulders from 635 000 NAC images, 60°S–60°N. https://eos.org/editor-highlights/nearly-94-million-boulders-mapped-on-the-moon-using-deep-learning
- Chang'e craters (Yang et al., Nat. Commun. 11, 6358, 2020): 109 956 new craters; ages for 18 996 craters > 8 km; seed 7 895 + 1 411 dated craters; transfer learning. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7755906
- Mars 90 million craters (Lagain et al., Nat. Commun. 12, 6352, 2021): CDA trained on HiRISE, run on global CTX mosaic; Tooting and 09-000015 as depleted-shergottite sources. https://horizon.documentation.ird.fr/exl-doc/pleins_textes/2021-12/010083348.pdf
- HORUS (Bickel et al., Nat. Commun. 2021): deep-learning denoising of NAC images of PSRs, ~1.5 m/pixel; 17 PSRs; later VIPER support (20 regions), 44 PSRs. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8460740
- PIXL adaptive sampling (JPL news 16 Jul 2024): autonomous placement via hexapod; scans for carbonates/phosphates and triggers "long dwell"; first instrument on Mars to use adaptive sampling. https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/heres-how-ai-is-changing-nasas-mars-rover-science
- MER WATCH: dust-devil and cloud detection uploaded 2006; first onboard dust-devil detection on Spirit sol 1147 (Apr 2007); 75 % data reduction on dust-devil sequences; cloud >90 % accuracy. https://ai.jpl.nasa.gov/public/projects/watch-spotter
- AI4Mars (Swan et al., CVPRW 2021): ~326 000 crowd labels on ~35 000 images (Curiosity, Opportunity, Spirit), 10 labels per image; DeepLabv3 >96 % accuracy. https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/ai4mars-a-dataset-for-terrain-aware-autonomous-driving-on-mars
- CRISM retired 3 Apr 2023 (cryocoolers exhausted 2017). https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasa-retires-mineral-mapping-instrument-on-mars-orbiter/
- Fresh-impact classifier (2020) and AEGIS counts are in chapters 44 and 48 (cross-referenced, not repeated).

## Human spaceflight
- CIMON: on ISS 2 Jul 2018 – 27 Aug 2019 (first use with Gerst 15 Nov 2018); CIMON-2 launched 5 Dec 2019 (CRS-19), emotion analysis; ICHIBAN demo July 2025: Takuya Onishi used CIMON (voice, via ground AI server) to command JAXA's Int-Ball2 camera drone in Kibo (NASA photo iss073e0384102, 18 Jul 2025). https://www.dlr.de/en/latest/news/2019/03/20190828_cimon-back-on-earth-after-14-months ; https://www.theregister.com/2025/08/05/germany_and_japan_teamed_their/
- Callisto (Lockheed Martin, Amazon Alexa offline, Cisco Webex) flew on Artemis I, Nov–Dec 2022; not planned for Artemis II. https://newsroom.cisco.com/c/r/newsroom/en/us/a/y2022/m01/lockheed-martin-amazon-and-cisco-to-bring-voice-technology-and-video-collaboration-to-the-moon.html
- Booz Allen LLM on HPE Spaceborne Computer-2 (Aug 2024); "Space Llama" (fine-tuned Llama 3.2) deployed on ISS from 25 Apr 2025. https://about.fb.com/news/2025/04/space-llama-metas-open-source-ai-model-heading-into-orbit/
- Wukong AI (China): delivered to Tiangong by Tianzhou-9, 15 Jul 2025; onboard + ground modules; used to prepare a spacewalk (Aug 2025). https://www.theregister.com/2025/08/19/china_wukong_ai_space_station/
- NASA–Google Crew Medical Officer Digital Assistant (CMO-DA), Vertex AI, multimodal, offline-capable; OSCE-graded tests: ankle injury 88 %, ear pain 80 %, flank pain 74 % (Aug 2025); not operational. https://dev.aiwire.net/2025/08/19/google-and-nasa-pilot-an-ai-medical-assistant-for-deep-space/
- ESA ALISSE (GMV, Mar 2024): AI-guided ultrasound, kidneys and bladder; 9 of 10 student images clinically acceptable; prototype. https://space-travel.com/just-add-ai-for-expert-astronaut-ultrasound-999/
- CSA Bio-Monitor (Astroskin, Carré Technologies): ISS from Dec 2018/Jan 2019 (Saint-Jacques), ECG, breathing, skin temperature, blood pressure, SpO2. https://www.csa-asc.gc.ca/eng/sciences/astroskin.asp
- Artemis II ARCHeR: wrist-worn actigraphs (motion, sleep, light) on all four crew, Apr 2026. https://www.nasa.gov/?p=946592
- Columbus ECLSS AI toolchain (Tappe & Moddemann, Aug 2025): supervised anomaly detection, diagnosis, reconfiguration, integrated at Airbus; ECLS >1 000 signals at 1 Hz; Columbus ~17 000 telemetry parameters (2020 Airbus/TFX autoencoders). https://www.esa.int/Enabling_Support/Preparing_for_the_Future/Discovery_and_Preparation/Using_AI_for_more_reliable_space_missions ; https://blog.tensorflow.org/2020/04/how-airbus-detects-anomalies-iss-telemetry-data-tfx.html
- NASA Integrated Medical Model: Monte Carlo over 100 conditions, medical-kit optimisation; IMPACT for exploration missions (Artemis II comparison, AsMA 2025). https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-integrated-medical-model-imm-overview/
- CHAPEA 2: 19 Oct 2025 – 31 Oct 2026 (Elder, Ellis, Montgomery, Spicer). https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/chapea
- Project Sidekick (HoloLens) delivered to ISS Dec 2015 (OA-4); Scott Kelly photo 20 Feb 2016. https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Project_Sidekick
