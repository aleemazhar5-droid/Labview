## Design
- NASA ST5: three microsatellites launched 22 Mar 2006 (Pegasus XL); X-band antennas evolved by J. Lohn (NASA Ames), G. Hornby (UCSC), D. Linden (JEM Engineering); first computer-evolved hardware in space; after an orbit change, new antennas evolved, built and tested within about one month; performance comparable to the contractor's hand design. https://www.techbriefs.com/component/content/article/8068-arc-15568-1 ; https://gpbib.cs.ucl.ac.uk/gp-html/lohn_2004_GPTP.html (checked 1 Nov 2026)
- NASA Goddard "evolved structures" (R. McClelland): commercial generative-design software; up to two-thirds lighter; design in 1–2 hours, parts in about a week; used on EXCITE balloon telescope, Mars Sample Return (CCRS diode mount), balloon observatories; human review needed ("algorithm can make structures too thin"). NASA article 9 Feb 2023 (updated 2025–26). https://www.nasa.gov/technology/goddard-tech/nasa-turns-to-ai-to-design-mission-hardware/ ; https://etd.gsfc.nasa.gov/our-work/ai-bones-evolved-structures-for-future-explorationgn-mission-hardware
- EXCITE tip/tilt bracket (McClelland, NASA AI Summit Oct 2023, NTRS 20230013964): loads 10 g vertical, 3 g lateral on 1.35 kg stage; >100 Hz; mass target 0.2 kg. Best manufacturable human design 0.27 kg, 65 Hz, 103 MPa, 2 days; AI (CNC) 0.20 kg, 147 Hz, 14.8 MPa, 1 h, 31 iterations, $1 000, 3 days; AI (laser powder-bed AM) 0.20 kg, 177 Hz, 11.2 MPa, $2 000, 3 weeks. Process: encode requirements ~1 h, evolve ~1 h; SIMP/level-set topology optimisation underneath; "2×–4× lighter, requirements to parts in < 2 weeks". Other parts: NGXO mirror mount, STAR-X detector bench, ALICE optical bench, HERMES mag stanchion, Dragonfly carousel base. https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20230013964/downloads/Evolved%20Structures%20-%20AI%20Summit%202023_10_17_sti.pdf
- EXCITE engineering test flight 31 Aug 2024 from Columbia Scientific Balloon Facility, Fort Sumner NM (NASA photos GSFC2024excite01–05).
- NASA digital engineering (INCOSE IS 2026 paper, NTRS 20260004382): since 2020 NASA deploys commercial ML-based generative design tools; NASA DE team used commercial LLMs to draft enterprise runbooks/procedures, "saved months", treated as drafts to verify. https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20260004382/downloads/INCOSE%20IS%2026%20NASA%20Digital%20Engineering%20final.pdf
- LEAP 71 (Dubai), Noyron computational model: first hot fire June 2024 (5 kN kerosene/LOX, Airborne Engineering, Westcott UK, 3.5 s); aerospike ~5 kN fired 18 Dec 2024; Dec 2025: two 20 kN methalox engines (bell and aerospike), CuCrZr printed, spec to hot fire < 3 weeks; bell >93 % combustion efficiency; aerospike reached 50 bar but start-transient issues; 200 kN and 2 000 kN designs in manufacturing validation; plans for 2026. https://3dprint.com/322814/leap-71-tests-two-3d-printed-methane-rocket-engines-in-weeks/ ; https://www.metal-am.com/leap-71-hot-fires-additively-manufactured-rocket-engine-designed-without-human-intervention/
- RUAG Space Sentinel-1 antenna bracket: topology optimised (Altair OptiStruct), printed in aluminium (EOS M 400), half the mass, stiffness requirement exceeded by > 30 %, qualified ~2016 (flight status unclear — say "qualified"). https://www.theengineer.co.uk/viewpoint-3d-printing-for-the-final-frontier/
- Simulation-AI funding: PhysicsX (London) $135 M Series B, 23 Jun 2025 (Atomico lead; >$155 M with Nov extension). Neural Concept (Lausanne) raised $100 M, 18 Dec 2025. https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/physicsx-raises-135m-series-b-usher-new-era-ai-native-engineering-and ; https://siliconangle.com/?p=725647
