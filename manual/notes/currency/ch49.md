# Chapter 49 — currency notes (facts checked October 2026)

All checked 2026-10-26 unless stated.

## Mission operations and telemetry
- JWST: ~36 000 telemetered points (mnemonics) + ~4 000 derived ground points = 40 000; recorded telemetry ~100 kb/s;
  sampling 0.064 s to >32 s; ~10 million samples per 28-min ingest file; LITA trending system built on Chandra's
  open-source tools. Source: Kauffman, Hunter, Arvai, SpaceOps-2025 paper 137,
  https://publications.spaceops.org/2025/download_by_id.php?id=0137
- Sentinel-3 (EUMETSAT): ~46 000 HKTM parameters routinely reported; flight control team assessed kNN and
  neural-network (autoencoder) outlier-detection tools on the EUMETSAT ML Framework; semi-supervised outlier detection
  already in routine reporting toolsets of multiple EUMETSAT missions for a subset of parameters; CHART framework
  (began on Metop); detected micrometeorite/debris impacts; key lessons: training-data definition, explainability,
  ground knowledge (operations events) to reduce false positives. Source: Trollope et al., SpaceOps-2025 paper 408,
  https://publications.spaceops.org/2025/download_by_id.php?id=0408
- Planet: IAC-18 paper — >200 Doves launched, ops by team of 8 engineers (5 Berlin, 3 San Francisco), >2 000 passes
  per day; daily health check a couple of hours. https://iafastro.directory/iac/archive/browse/IAC-18/B6/2/46924/
  June 2022 presentation (L. McGill, "How to effectively operate 100s of satellites"): Dove daily monitoring cut from
  ~4 h/day to ~30 min/day; SkySat night shifts and 24/7 support eliminated; nominal operations 100 % automated
  (pass commanding, contact scheduling, fleet reports, delivery). Automation = periodically triggered Python scripts.
  https://www.nasa.gov/sites/default/files/atoms/files/how_to_effectively_operate_100s_of_satellites_-_lessons_learned_from_planet_mission_operations.pdf
- Planet/RapidEye PROTOS (IAC-16): Kalman filter estimates battery depth of discharge (Coulomb counting + voltage);
  linearised fade model with temperature and DoD; mission life = date battery reaches 50 % of 15 Ah; genetic algorithm
  re-planned operations; ~15 % longer mission life. https://iafastro.directory/iac/archive/browse/IAC-16/B6/3/34579/
- Starlink collision avoidance: 148 696 manoeuvres Jun–Nov 2025 (44 559 Gen1, 104 137 Gen2) per FCC semiannual report;
  threshold Pc ≈ 3 × 10⁻⁷; Dec 2025–May 2026 reported 207 152 (Space.com, secondary).
  https://www.keeptrack.space/deep-dive/collision-avoidance-conjunction-screening ;
  https://space.com/space-exploration/satellites/every-spacex-starlink-satellite-has-to-dodge-a-collision-almost-weekly-and-experts-fear-the-worst

## Anomaly detection
- Telemanom (Hundman et al., KDD 2018): LSTM per channel, nonparametric dynamic threshold; SMAP + MSL 82 channels,
  105 labelled anomaly sequences; precision 87.5 %, recall 80 % (as in Chapter 43).
- ESA-ADB (Kotowski et al., arXiv 2406.17826, v1 25 Jun 2024, v2 Aug 2025; KP Labs + ESA/ESOC): ESA Anomalies Dataset
  from three missions, two in benchmark. Mission1: 76 channels (58 target), 14 years, 200 events (118 anomalies,
  78 rare nominal events, 4 gaps), 1.80 % annotated points. Mission2: 100 channels (47 target), 3.5 years, 644 events
  (31 anomalies, 613 rare nominal), 0.58 %. Total 1.55 billion points, 7.32 GB compressed, 844 events.
  Best: Telemanom-ESA-Pruned (Mission1; corrected event-wise F0.5 0.968 for anomalies only); Windowed iForest best on
  Mission2 lightweight subset (F0.5 0.949); no algorithm found the 9 actual anomalies hidden among rare nominal
  events in Mission2 full set; more channels → more false detections. https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.17826
- ESA-ADB follow-up (arXiv 2607.07335, July 2026): supervised CNN/GCN/GAT better, unsupervised (ECOD, Elliptic
  Envelope) competitive precision at much lower cost.
- DLR GSOC ATHMoS (2016–19): outlier detection based on intrinsic dimensionality, Historic Nominal / Recent Nominal /
  Anomaly databases, later neural-network telemetry prediction ~4.5 h ahead; main difficulty false positives.
  https://elib.dlr.de/105549 , https://elib.dlr.de/121211
- CNES NOSTRADAMUS: one-class SVM prototype (2014 R&D with TeSA) after 2013 trial of ESA Novelty Detection (kNN);
  developed to evaluate operationally; 2023 transfer learning for early-life satellites.
  https://www.tesa.prd.fr/documents/26/fuertes_17103.pdf
- NASA Ames IMS: selected ~2005 by ISS attitude control (gyroscopes/CMGs) in Houston; by 2012 applied to about a dozen
  MCC systems; licensed 2012 (CEMSol). https://spinoff.nasa.gov/node/10871
- AISHGO (GSOC + ESOC): AI for incident classification, predictive maintenance, anomaly detection, long-term health.

## Assistants and automation
- ESA OCAI (Operations CompAnIon): in use across 14 ESOC missions (SpaceOps 2025), search/correlation over TM/TC
  archives, procedures, anomaly reports, logs; BM25 keyword + embedding kNN + knowledge graph; LLM response generation
  being added; envisaged pilot in Telespazio EASE-Rise with constellr HiVE. SpaceOps-2025 paper 378,
  https://publications.spaceops.org/2025/download_by_id.php?id=0378
- Aerospace Corporation + Google Public Sector (6 Apr 2026): proof-of-concept agentic-AI tool for pLEO anomaly
  triage; correlates anomalies with payload tasks/space weather, proposes root causes; decision support.
  https://www.militaryaerospace.com/communications/news/55387527/aerospace-and-google-collaborate-on-ai-for-satellite-operations
- JPL DSN agentic anomaly detection in ground data systems (arXiv 2508.21111, Aug 2025): preliminary, false positives.
- Quindar: $18 M Series A Nov 2025; "AI autopilot" with manual override (Jan 2025 seed extension).
  https://washingtontechnology.com/companies/2025/11/quindar-closes-18m-series-round-classified-business-push/409631
- Dec 2025 Perseverance drives planned by vision-language models, checked on digital twin (500 000 telemetry
  variables) — in Chapter 44; refer.
- Research: LLM agents controlling spacecraft in Kerbal Space Program differential games (2nd place, 2024,
  arXiv 2404.00413).

## Scheduling and ground networks
- DSN: oversubscription routinely up to 40 %; weekly unmet demand ~300 h (2011–12) to ~500 h (late 2015)
  (ICAPS 2016, https://ai.jpl.nasa.gov/public/papers/shouraboura-icaps2016-dsn.pdf); ~370 tracks/week (2006);
  automated Service Scheduling Software (S3) with mixed-initiative; deep RL scheduler research (Goh et al., 2021,
  arXiv 2102.05167) and MILP (arXiv 2111.11628).
- Azure Orbital Ground Station retired 18 Dec 2024 (announced 3 Oct 2024); ten 6 m antennas sold to SLI, operated by
  RBC Signals. https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/microsoft-retires-ground-station-services-sells-antenna-to-sli/
- KSAT: ~300 antennas (2022); KSATlite >52 000 passes in March 2022. https://www.ksat.no/news/news-archive/2022/all-time-high-for-ksatlite-network/

## Communications and networks
- RadioML 2018.01A: 24 modulations, 2 555 904 examples of 1 024 samples, SNR −20 to +30 dB in 2 dB steps; ResNet ≈ 95 %
  at high SNR (O'Shea, Roy, Clancy 2018).
- SCaN Testbed (ISS): cognitive anti-jamming with RL-based channel selection, closed loop satellite-to-ground,
  WInnComm 2018 (Jayaweera et al.), NTRS 20190001915.
- Beam hopping: first over-the-air demonstration June 2018 (ESA BEHOP: Fraunhofer IIS, WORK Microwave, Eutelsat),
  DVB-S2X Annex E superframes. https://iis.fraunhofer.de/en/pr/2018/20180709_KS_Beam-Hopping.html
- Starlink lasers (SPIE Photonics West, 30 Jan 2024, T. Brashears): >9 000 lasers, >42 PB/day, 100 Gbit/s links,
  up to 5 400 km, ~99.99 % claimed uptime by rapid rerouting. https://hackaday.com/2024/02/05/starlinks-inter-satellite-laser-links-are-setting-new-record-with-42-million-gb-per-day/
- ESA SkyMon PIA (completed 2021): ML carrier identification, spectral anomaly detection, interference
  classification. https://connectivity.esa.int/projects/skymon-pia ; KTH/SSC LSTM interference detection (arXiv 1912.04716).
- 3GPP: NTN first in Rel-17 (2022); Rel-19 (2025) regenerative payloads, store-and-forward; 6G studies in Rel-20
  (incl. AI/ML air interface); first 6G specs expected ~2028–29 (Rel-21). ITU-R M.2160 IMT-2030 framework Nov 2023.
- DVB-S2 MODCOD thresholds: ETSI EN 302 307-1, Table 13 (ideal Es/N0, AWGN, normal frames).

## Security
- Viasat KA-SAT attack 24 Feb 2022: AcidRain wiper; tens of thousands of modems; 5 800 Enercon wind turbines lost
  remote monitoring; EU attributed to Russia 10 May 2022. https://www.sentinelone.com/labs/acidrain-a-modem-wiper-rains-down-on-europe/
- ML jamming detection for TM/TC links (Gecgel & Kurt 2021, arXiv 2107.06181).
- Hubble: payload computer anomaly June–July 2021 (backup hardware); one-gyro mode from June 2024 (12 % efficiency loss).
