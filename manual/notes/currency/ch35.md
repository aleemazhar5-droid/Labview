# Chapter 35 — Rocket Propulsion: currency notes (checked 18 Oct 2026)

Engine data (used in the engine table and the chamber-pressure figure)
- Raptor: SpaceX X post 3 Aug 2024 — Raptor 3 (sea-level) 280 tf, Isp 350 s, 1 525 kg; Raptor 2 230 tf / 1 630 kg; Raptor 1 185 tf / 2 080 kg. In 2026 (Starship V3 update) SpaceX said Raptor 3 nominally produces 250 tf (275 tf vacuum variant); operational chamber pressure "up to 330 bar" (350 bar reached in tests, 2023). Raptor 2 Pc 300 bar. Mixture ratio 3.6. RVac Isp ≈ 380 s. Raptor 3 first flew on Starship Flight 12, 22 May 2026 (one of 33 booster engines shut down early; boostback incomplete). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SpaceX_Raptor (checked 18 Oct 2026); https://spaceq.ca/starship-flight-12-validates-v3-reentry-systems-despite-early-booster-termination/
- Starship Flight 14 (28 Sep 2026) first orbital flight — from notes/currency_2026.md.
- Merlin 1D: 845 kN SL / 914 kN vac(first-stage engine; Falcon 9 7 607 kN SL), Isp 282/311 s, Pc 9.7 MPa, 470 kg, T/W ≈ 184; MVac 981 kN, 348 s, ε 165. MVac failed during relight on Starlink 9-3, 11 Jul 2024. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SpaceX_Merlin (18 Oct 2026)
- Falcon 9 reuse record: B1067 flew its 37th flight on 25 Aug 2026. https://aiaa.org/2026/08/25/spacex-falcon-9-booster-sets-reuse-record-with-37th-flight/
- RS-25 (L3Harris spec sheet, 07/2024): 109 % power: 418 000 lbf SL (1 859 kN), 512 300 lbf vac (2 279 kN); throttle 67–109 %; Pc 2 994 psia (20.6 MPa); Isp vac 452.3 s; HPFTP 71 140 hp, HPOTP 23 260 hp; ε 69; dry 7 774 lb (3 526 kg); MR 6.03; >1.1 million s of testing; 16 shuttle engines for first four Artemis flights; new engines (Artemis V onward) ~30 % cheaper, run to 111 %. https://l3harris.com/sites/default/files/2024-07/l3harris-ar-rs-25-spec-sheet.pdf
- RS-25 production-engine certification: 12 hot fires Feb–Jun 2023 on the Fred Haise Test Stand (Stennis), 80–111 % and some time at 113 %. https://www.l3harris.com/newsroom/press-release/2023/06/initial-rs-25-certification-campaign-12-hot-fire-tests-complete
- BE-4: ox-rich staged combustion, single preburner, LOX/LNG; 2 400 kN (550 klbf) original; Nov 2025 upgrade to ~2 847 kN (640 klbf); Pc ≈ 13.4–14 MPa; throttle 40–100 %; Isp ~340 s (vac, listed); first flight Vulcan 8 Jan 2024; BE-4 exploded in test 30 Jun 2023. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BE-4
- New Glenn pad explosion 28 May 2026 during hot fire before NG-4: Blue Origin (D. Limp, Aug 2026) traced it to the main oxygen valve of one BE-4. https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/08/06/blue-origin-identifies-engine-issue-behind-new-glenn-explosion/
- BE-3U: expander-bleed (open expander) LOX/LH2; original 711.5 kN vac, raised to ~778 kN (Apr 2025) and ~941 kN demonstrated (Nov 2025); Isp ~445 s. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BE-3
- RL10C-1-1: 105.98 kN, 453.8 s, ε 155, 188 kg (415 lb); RL10B-2 110.1 kN, 465.5 s, ε 280; RL10C-X → production RL10E-1 (107.3 kN, 460.9 s). First flight Nov 1963. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RL10 ; L3Harris RL10 page.
- RD-180: 3 830/4 150 kN, 311/338 s, Pc 26.7 MPa, 5 480 kg, MR 2.72, ox-rich SC, 2 chambers. Russia halted supply/maintenance 2022; Atlas V being retired. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RD-180
- RD-191: 1 920/2 090 kN, 310.7/337 s, Pc 25.8 MPa, 2 290 kg, MR 2.6, throttle 27–105 %. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RD-191
- RD-170: Pc 24.5 MPa, 7 250/7 900 kN, 309/337 s. RD-171MV first flew on Soyuz-5 demo, 30 Apr 2026 (suborbital). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RD-170 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soyuz-5_(rocket)
- RD-253: Pc 14.7 MPa, NTO/UDMH ox-rich SC, 1965. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RD-253
- Vulcain 2.1: gas generator, ~1 371 kN vac (ESA; Wikipedia lists 1 324 kN), Isp ≈ 432–434 s vac, Pc ≈ 11.7–12 MPa; Vulcain 2 Pc 117 bar, MR 6.1, ε 58. Nozzle with 90 % fewer parts. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulcain_(rocket_engine)
- Vinci: expander, 180 kN, 457 s, Pc 60 bar, ε 240, MR 6.1, multiple restarts; first flight 9 Jul 2024 (APU anomaly prevented third burn). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinci_(rocket_engine)
- Prometheus: LOX/CH4 gas generator, ~980 kN (100 t class), Pc 100 bar, 30–110 % throttle, up to 50 % 3D-printed, €1 M cost target; first start Nov 2022; four consecutive ignitions 20 Jun 2025. Themis T1H hop vehicle at Esrange; wet dress rehearsal 23 Jul 2026; hop test pending. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prometheus_(rocket_engine) ; https://europeanspaceflight.com/esa-waiting-for-spring-to-launch-themis-reusable-rocket-demonstrator/
- P120C: ~142 t propellant, ~4 500 kN max, ~135 s, carbon-fibre monolithic case. P160C: 156 t, ~4 780 kN max; first flight Ariane 6 VA269 17 Jun 2026 (36 Amazon Leo sats). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P160C ; https://www.esa.int/esatv/Videos/2026/06/First_Ariane_6_liftoff_with_most_powerful_boosters
- YF-100: 1 200/1 340 kN, 300/335 s, Pc 18 MPa, ox-rich SC LOX/RP-1, ε 35. YF-100K uprated (LM-10, LM-12). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YF-100
- YF-77: 518/700 kN, 316.7/428 s, Pc 10.1 MPa, gas generator, MR 5.5, ε 49. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YF-77
- TQ-12: LOX/CH4 gas generator, 658 kN SL (670 kN design), 284.5/337 s, Pc 10.1 MPa, ε 45; TQ-12A +9 % thrust. Zhuque-2 first methalox rocket to orbit (12 Jul 2023). Zhuque-3 (9 × TQ-12A, stainless steel): flight 1 3 Dec 2025 orbit, landing failed; flight 2 18 Aug 2026, Honghu-03 to orbit, booster landed ~390 km downrange but tipped over (leg failure, fire). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TQ-12 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhuque-3
- LE-9: expander bleed LOX/LH2, 1 471 kN vac, 426 s, Pc 10 MPa, MR 5.9, ε 37, 2.4 t. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LE-9
- Rutherford: electric-pump-fed LOX/RP-1, ~25 kN SL / 26 kN vac, Isp 311/343 s, 35 kg; two 37 kW BLDC motors at ~40 000 rpm; LiPo battery; mostly 3D-printed; first flight 25 May 2017. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutherford_(rocket_engine)
- Archimedes: ox-rich SC LOX/CH4, 730 kN SL / 890 kN vac, 329/365 s, Pc ~100 bar, throttle 50–100 %; first hot fire 8 Aug 2024. Neutron first launch delayed to 2027 (announced 14–15 Oct 2026) after voids found in the composite forward tank dome; a stage-1 tank ruptured in a hydrostatic test in Jan 2026. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archimedes_(rocket_engine) ; https://www.satellitetoday.com/launch/2026/10/15/rocket-labs-neutron-debut-now-delayed-to-2027/
- Vikas: UH25/N2O4 gas generator, ~800 kN class (737/821 kN SL/vac), Pc 5.85 MPa, Isp ≈ 263/293 s. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vikas_(rocket_engine)
- CE-20: LOX/LH2 gas generator, 186–200 kN (19–22 t ratings), 442 s, Pc 6 MPa, MR 5.05, ε 100, 588 kg; human-rated Feb 2024; in-flight restart on LVM3-M5 (Nov 2025); 22 t hot tests 10 Mar, 6 Jul and 9 Sep 2026 for the C32 stage. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CE-20
- F-1: 6 770/7 770 kN, 263/304 s, Pc 7 MPa, 8 400 kg, MR 2.27, 55 000 bhp turbine; 65 flown on 13 Saturn V. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocketdyne_F-1
- F-1 instability: 28 Jun 1962 destructive instability; Project Go; Rocketdyne ran ~2 000 tests of 210 injector/baffle variants (15 baffle designs, 14 injector configurations); bomb tests — instability damped within 45 ms (McCutcheon, enginehistory.org 2021); NASA: "more than three years", copper baffles. https://enginehistory.org/Rockets/RPE08.11/RPE08.11.shtml ; https://www.nasa.gov/?p=97594
- V-2: Pc ≈ 1.5 MPa, ~25 t thrust, ethanol-water/LOX, H2O2 steam turbine, regenerative + film cooling. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V-2_rocket

Solids and hybrids
- Shuttle SRB: ~12 MN at lift-off, max ~14.7 MN; 242/268 s; 123 s; ~500 t propellant; AP 69.8 %, Al 16 %, PBAN 12.04 %, epoxy 1.96 %, Fe2O3 0.2 %; 11-point star forward segment; D6AC steel case; thrust reduced ~1/3 around max-Q; Challenger 28 Jan 1986, 36 °F; RSRM added third O-ring. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Shuttle_Solid_Rocket_Booster
- SLS booster (NASA): 5 segments, 3.6 Mlbf (16 MN) each, 126 s, PBAN, 1.6 Mlb, >75 % of lift-off thrust, single-use. https://www.nasa.gov/reference/space-launch-system-solid-rocket-booster/
- BOLE DM-1 static test 26 Jun 2025, Promontory, ~2 min 20 s; nozzle anomaly near end of burn. https://www.nasaspaceflight.com/2025/06/bole-dm1-test/
- GEM 63XL: nozzle lost on Vulcan Cert-2 (4 Oct 2024); second nozzle anomaly on USSF-87 (12 Feb 2026); Space Force paused NSSL Vulcan flights; GEM 63XL static test with nozzle enhancements 15 Apr 2026; no Vulcan return-to-flight as of late Aug 2026 (goal: end of 2026). https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/05/14/ula-confirms-successful-solid-rocket-booster-test-as-vulcan-anomaly-investigation-continues/
- Nammo Nucleus hybrid (H2O2/HTPB, ~30 kN) reached ~107 km from Andøya 27 Sep 2018. https://www.esa.int/Enabling_Support/Space_Transportation/Norway_takes_the_lead_in_hybrid_propulsion
- Gilmour Eris (hybrid) first flight 30 Jul 2025 (UTC) from Bowen, failed after ~14 s; second flight expected end-2026. https://www.spaceconnectonline.com.au/launch/7019-gilmour-confirms-expectation-to-conduct-second-test-flight-in-late-2026
- RocketMotorTwo: N2O + solid polymer fuel (HTPB / polyamide), ~310–320 kN, ~60 s. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RocketMotorTwo ; https://airandspace.si.edu/collection-objects/rocket-engine-hybrid-rocketmotortwo/nasm_A20190352000
