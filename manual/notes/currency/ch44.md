# Chapter 44 — Deep Learning, Foundation Models and Generative AI: currency and fact notes (checked 25 Oct 2026)

Earth and space foundation models
- Prithvi (Prithvi-EO-1.0, NASA–IBM): released openly on Hugging Face 3 Aug 2023; ~100 M parameters; temporal ViT masked autoencoder; pre-trained on >1 TB of HLS (Harmonized Landsat Sentinel-2, L30, 30 m, six bands) over the contiguous US (one year); fine-tuned for flood mapping (Sen1Floods11), burn scars, multi-temporal crop segmentation, cloud-gap imputation; IBM claim "15 % improvement over state of the art with half the labelled data" (vendor claim); Apache-2.0. https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.18660 ; https://huggingface.co/ibm-nasa-geospatial/Prithvi-EO-1.0-100M ; https://www.satellitetoday.com/imagery-and-sensing/2023/08/04/ibm-and-nasa-release-open-source-geospatial-ai-foundation-model/ ; https://earthdata.nasa.gov/news/impact-ibm-hls-foundation-model
- Prithvi-EO-2.0 (arXiv 3 Dec 2024, v3 6 Mar 2026): 300 M (ViT-L) and 600 M (ViT-H, 14×14 patches) + TL variants with time/location embeddings; ~4.2 M global HLS samples (256×256, four timestamps, 2014–2023); 400 epochs on JUWELS Booster (JSC), A100 40 GB: 300M ≈ 21 000 GPU-h on 80 GPUs, 600M ≈ 58 000 GPU-h on 240 GPUs; +8 % vs Prithvi-EO-1.0 on GEO-Bench (abstract); few-label landslide (~1 % of data, ~50 images): 67.0 mIoU vs 59.7 U-Net; burn scars 90.5 mIoU (600M) vs 86.9 (1.0); floods 90.3 vs 88.3 mIoU; Apache-2.0. https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.02732 ; https://arxiv.org/html/2412.02732v3 ; https://huggingface.co/ibm-nasa-geospatial/Prithvi-EO-2.0-600M
- Prithvi WxC (NASA–IBM, arXiv 2409.13598, Sept 2024): 2.3 B parameters; 160 MERRA-2 variables (40 years); encoder–decoder transformer, mixed masked-reconstruction/forecast objective; downstream: rollout forecasting, downscaling, gravity-wave flux parameterisation, extreme events; weights licence CDLA-Permissive-2.0 on Hugging Face (code MIT). https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.13598v1 ; https://huggingface.co/ibm-nasa-geospatial/Prithvi-WxC-1.0-2300M
- Clay v1.5 (Clay Foundation, weights 19 Nov 2024): 632 M total (encoder 311 M ViT-L-size MAE, dim 1024, depth 24; decoder 15 M; DINOv2 teacher 304 M); patch 8, input 256, 75 % masking; ~70 M chips from Sentinel-2, Sentinel-1, Landsat 8/9, NAIP, LINZ, MODIS; trained Sept 2024 on 20 AWS g6.48xlarge (8 L4 each = 160 GPUs) for ~100 epochs × ~8 h; Apache-2.0 code and weights. https://clay-foundation.github.io/model/release-notes/specification.html
- SatMAE (Stanford, NeurIPS 2022): MAE for temporal and multispectral imagery (time embeddings, independent masking across time, grouped spectral encodings); ViT-L checkpoints on fMoW (top-1 77.8 % non-temporal, 80.0 % temporal) and fMoW-Sentinel (63.8 %); gains up to 7 % supervised and 14 % transfer (abstract). https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/01c561df365429f33fcd7a7faa44c985-Abstract.html ; https://github.com/sustainlab-group/SatMAE
- SkySense (Ant Group, Wuhan Univ., CVPR 2024): 2.06 B parameters (Swin-H for HR optical, ViT-L for multispectral and SAR); 21.5 M temporal sequences (optical + SAR); multi-granularity contrastive learning; 16 datasets, 7 tasks; weights for non-commercial research only. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.10115 ; https://github.com/Jack-bo1220/SkySense ; SkySense V2 ICCV 2025.
- TerraMind (IBM, ESA Φ-lab, Forschungszentrum Jülich; arXiv 2504.11171, 15 Apr 2025; ICCV 2025): first "any-to-any" generative multimodal EO FM; ~500 B tokens from ~9 M aligned samples of TerraMesh; modalities incl. S2 L1C/L2A, S1 GRD/RTC, DEM, LULC, NDVI, captions, geolocation; v1-B pre-trained on 32 A100 for 6 (main text) or 12 days (supplement: 9 216 GPU-h), v1-L 10 days on 32 A100; PANGAEA avg mIoU: TerraMind-L 59.57, -B 58.35, task-specific U-Net 57.58, CROMA 55.29, Prithvi 1.0 100M 51.00 (only TerraMind beats U-Net); Apache-2.0; lighter edge versions Oct 2025. https://arxiv.org/html/2504.11171 ; https://huggingface.co/ibm-esa-geospatial/TerraMind-1.0-large ; https://research.ibm.com/blog/terramind-esa-earth-observation-model
- AlphaEarth Foundations (Google DeepMind, 30 Jul 2025, arXiv 2507.22291): embedding field model; 64-dimensional embedding per 10 m pixel; annual layers 2017–2024 released as "Satellite Embedding dataset" in Google Earth Engine; inputs optical (Sentinel-2, Landsat), radar (Sentinel-1, PALSAR-2), lidar (GEDI), climate simulations etc.; ~24 % lower error than other approaches tested; 16× less storage; >1.4 trillion embedding footprints per year; >50 organisations tested. Jul 2026: "Custom Satellite Embeddings" private preview. https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/alphaearth-foundations-helps-map-our-planet-in-unprecedented-detail/ ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22291
- Aurora (Microsoft; Nature 641, 1180–1187, 29 May 2025, Bodnar et al.): 1.3 B parameters; 3D Perceiver encoder/decoder + 3D Swin transformer processor; pre-trained on >1 million hours of geophysical data; fine-tuned (incl. LoRA for roll-out) for air quality, ocean waves, tropical-cyclone tracks (beat seven operational centres on 5-day tracks), 0.1° weather; ~5 000× faster than IFS (estimate); pre-training ~2.5 weeks on 32 GPUs (press); weights MIT licence on Hugging Face. https://ideas.repec.org/a/nat/nature/v641y2025i8065d10.1038_s41586-025-09005-y.html ; https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/introducing-aurora/ ; https://huggingface.co/microsoft/aurora ; https://foxweather.com/weather-news/ai-weather-forecast-aurora-faster
- Surya (NASA–IBM heliophysics FM; arXiv 2508.14112 submitted 18 Aug 2025; released ~20 Aug 2025): 366 M parameters; spatiotemporal transformer (spectral gating + long–short attention); ~9 years (2010–2019, ~218 TB) of SDO data, 8 AIA channels + 5 HMI products at 4096×4096; zero-shot solar-dynamics and flare forecasting; LoRA fine-tuning for solar wind, active-region segmentation (IoU 0.768, Dice 0.853 > U-Net), flare forecasting (~15–16 % better in preliminary tests, developers' figure), EUV spectra; Apache-2.0 weights. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14112 ; https://huggingface.co/nasa-ibm-ai4science/Surya-1.0 ; https://www.space.com/astronomy/sun/meet-surya-the-1st-of-its-kind-ai-model-nasa-and-ibm-built-to-predict-solar-storms
- OlmoEarth (Allen Institute for AI, 4 Nov 2025): open family Nano ~1.4 M, Tiny ~6.2 M, Base ~90 M, Large ~300 M; ~10 TB, millions of samples at 10 m, up to 12 monthly time steps; optical, radar, map layers. https://allenai.org/blog/olmoearth-models

Weather/ML models referenced (details belong to Chapter 47)
- GraphCast (Lam et al., Science, 14 Nov 2023): 0.25°, 37 levels; encode–process–decode GNN, 36.7 M parameters; multimesh = icosahedron refined 6 times → 40 962 nodes, 81 920 faces, edges of all levels superimposed (327 660 directed mesh edges, consistent with 2 × Σ 30·4^r); 16 message-passing layers; ERA5 1979–2017; ~4 weeks on 32 TPU v4; 10-day forecast < 1 min; beats HRES on 90 % of 1 380 targets. https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.12794 ; https://ar5iv.org/html/2212.12794 ; https://deepmind.google/research/publications/graphcast-learned-global-weather-forecasting/
- GenCast (Price et al., Nature, 4 Dec 2024): diffusion model on the sphere, 0.25°, 15-day ensembles of 50+ members; trained on ERA5 to 2018, tested 2019; more skilful than ECMWF ENS on 97.2 % of 1 320 targets (99.8 % beyond 36 h); one 15-day member ≈ 8 min on one TPU v5. https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/gencast-predicts-weather-and-the-risks-of-extreme-conditions-with-sota-accuracy/
- ECMWF AIFS: attention-based GNN encoder/decoder + sliding-window transformer processor; AIFS Single operational 25 Feb 2025, ENS 1 Jul 2025 (shared fact sheet). https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.01465
- CorrDiff (NVIDIA, arXiv 2309.15214): UNet mean + diffusion correction, downscales 25 km ERA5 to 2 km over Taiwan (CWA WRF targets). https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15214v4

Deep learning in space (examples)
- DeepMoon (Silburt et al., Icarus 2019, arXiv 1803.02192): U-Net crater detection on lunar DEMs; recovers 92 % of test-set craters, nearly doubles detections; ~11 % false positives among new craters (361 inspected); transfers to Mercury. https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.02192v2
- JPL automated fresh-impact crater classifier (announced 1 Oct 2020): trained on 6 830 CTX images; run on ~112 000 CTX images on JPL cluster (up to 750 copies in parallel); ~5 s per image vs ~40 min for a human; first AI-found cluster (Noctis Fossae, craters ~4 m, formed Mar 2010–May 2012) confirmed by HiRISE 26 Aug 2020; >20 more candidates. https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/ai-is-helping-scientists-discover-fresh-craters-on-mars/ ; photo PIA24040
- AI4Mars (JPL, CVPR-W 2021): ~326 000 crowdsourced semantic labels on ~35 000 images (Curiosity, Opportunity, Spirit), ~1 500 expert validation labels; DeepLabv3 >96 % overall test accuracy; later v0.6 ~425 000 labels / ~50 000 images; SPOC terrain classifier used in Curiosity ground ops. https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021W/AI4Space/papers/Swan_AI4MARS_A_Dataset_for_Terrain-Aware_Autonomous_Driving_on_Mars_CVPRW_2021_paper.pdf ; photo PIA23897
- Ultralytics YOLO26: unveiled Sept 2025 (YOLO Vision 2025), formally released 14 Jan 2026 (v8.4.0); NMS-free end-to-end; vendor claims up to 43 % faster CPU inference. https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260114168538/en ; https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.25164v1
- Pandey et al. 2023 (arXiv 2308.15712): AlexNet-based full-disk magnetogram flare model (≥M within 24 h), TSS ≈ 0.51; Guided Grad-CAM shows attention to active regions including near-limb. Related 2023 comparison of Guided Grad-CAM, Deep SHAP, Integrated Gradients (arXiv 2308.02682). https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15712
- Licata & Mehta 2022 (Space Weather): HASDM-ML thermospheric density model with UQ (PCA + NN, losses MSE/NLPD/CRPS); MC dropout in Sci. Rep. 2022. https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.07651v1 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.02067v1
- Martin & Schaub PINN gravity model (PINN-GM-III, J. Astronaut. Sci. 2025; arXiv 2312.10257): network maps position to potential, acceleration by automatic differentiation; addresses extrapolation error; Eros tests. https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10257v2
- Conformal prediction for full-disk flare intensity regression (arXiv 2603.06712, Mar 2026): conformalised quantile regression gave best coverage/shortest intervals. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06712v1

Language models
- INDUS (NASA–IBM, June 2024): encoder and sentence-embedding models; 60 B-token corpus across Earth science, astrophysics, planetary science, heliophysics, biological and physical sciences; custom 50 000-word tokenizer (over half domain-specific); sentence models fine-tuned on ~268 M text pairs; on Hugging Face. https://astrobiology.com/2024/07/14/nasa-ibm-collaboration-develops-indus-large-language-models-for-advanced-science-research/
- ESA EVE (Earth Virtual Expert; ESA Φ-lab with Pi School, project launched Jul 2024; arXiv 2604.13071, Mar 2026, ACL 2026 Industry): 24 B-parameter domain-adapted instruction model built on an open-weight general model; RAG + hallucination-detection pipeline; production API/GUI with 350 pilot users (Mar 2026); open-licence release planned; public use announced 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13071 ; https://eve.philab.esa.int/news-item/launch-of-the-earth-virtual-expert-project
- Perseverance: first drives with waypoints planned by commercial vision–language models, 8 Dec 2025 (210 m) and 10 Dec 2025 (246 m); commands checked on JPL digital twin (>500 000 telemetry variables); JPL release 30 Jan 2026. (Model/vendor not named in text per style rule.) https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasas-perseverance-rover-completes-first-ai-planned-drive-on-mars/
- Context windows 2026: leading commercial models ≈ 1 M tokens; some open models advertise more; effective context often well below advertised. https://www.morphllm.com/largest-context-window-llm (secondary; used only qualitatively)
- Magesh et al., J. Empirical Legal Studies 22:216–242 (2025): RAG-based legal research tools still hallucinated on 17–33 % of queries (tested May 2024). https://law.stanford.edu/publications/hallucination-free-assessing-the-reliability-of-leading-ai-legal-research-tools

- Prithvi in orbit: preprint "First On-Orbit Demonstration of a Geospatial Foundation Model" (Du et al., arXiv 2512.01181, 1 Dec 2025): Prithvi-EO-2.0-300M distilled ("dual-MAE") to a compact ViT (embedding 1024 → 256), 1.2 GB → 73 MB (~16× smaller), FP16 for Myriad-2; five tasks (cloud classification and segmentation, flood, landslide, above-ground biomass); accuracy within ~1 percentage point (cloud), AGB RMSE within 3 %; on-orbit inference on Thales Alenia Space IMAGIN-e (ISS, ARM Cortex-A72 CPU) with an uploaded test set; Kanyini (HyperScout-2, Myriad-2) results from engineering model on ground (~5.4–5.7 s per tile, ~7.2 W peak). NASA article 7 May 2026 (updated 13 May): "first geospatial foundation model deployed in orbit", Kanyini + IMAGIN-e, Adelaide University, ESA Φ-lab, Thales Alenia Space, SmartSat CRC. https://arxiv.org/html/2512.01181 ; https://science.nasa.gov/science-research/ai-foundation-model-in-orbit/
- NASA-IBM Lunar Foundation Model (released 10 Sep 2026, Prithvi family): ViT-B-size encoder–decoder (width 768, 12 layers) trained from scratch with TerraMind-style masked multimodal tokens; SomBench ≈ 2 M co-registered tile bundles (LROC WAC/NAC, LOLA, Diviner, Mini-RF, Kaguya/SELENE, GRAIL, Lunar Prospector, USGS); pre-training 16 H100, ~1.1 k GPU-h; polar-ice prospectivity RMSE 0.0293 vs 0.0377 SwinV2-B (≈ 22 % lower); craters and irregular mare patches comparable to best baselines; Apache-2.0; not validated for operational decisions. https://huggingface.co/nasa-ibm-ai4science/NASA-IBM-Lunar-Foundation-Model ; https://www.placera.se/pressmeddelanden/ibm-ibm-and-nasa-release-open-source-ai-model-to-support-lunar-exploration-20260910
