# Chapter 48 — currency notes (checked October 2026)

- ECSS-E-ST-70-11C (2008; Rev.1 published 15 Oct 2025) on-board autonomy levels: execution E1–E4 (E1 ground control
  with time-tagged safety commands; E2 pre-planned operations in onboard scheduler; E3 adaptive, event-based operations
  and onboard procedures; E4 goal-oriented onboard re-planning); mission data management D1–D2 only (D1 essential data
  stored onboard after ground outage or failure; D2 all mission data stored, independent of ground); fault management
  F1–F2 (F1 safe configuration after failure; F2 re-establish nominal operations). NB: no D3/D4 exist.
  Sources: https://ecss.nl/standard/ecss-e-st-70-11c-rev-1-space-segment-operability-15-october-2025/ ;
  SpaceOps 2010 "Taking the ECSS Autonomy Concepts One Step Further" (INPE) quoting D1/D2 text; checked 2026-10-26.
- Starlink FCC semi-annual report, 1 Dec 2025 – 31 May 2026: Gen 1 65 137 + Gen 2 142 015 = 207 152 propulsive
  collision-avoidance manoeuvres; ~36 and ~46 per satellite per year; threshold Pc > 3e-7 (industry 1e-4) plus
  miss-distance criteria; "onboard collision avoidance system"; 6 (Gen 1) + 16 (Gen 2) "outage" events, mostly
  poor secondary predictions, launch screening gap, Space-Track outages delaying CDMs. Previous period (Jun–Nov 2025):
  148 696. https://api-prod.fcc.gov/icfs-attachment/exp/api/v1/bff0244acfb947505791bf252f851ce6 ; Space.com
  (Jul 2026). Checked 2026-10-26.
- Perseverance: passed Opportunity's off-world distance record on 11 Oct 2026 (sol 2 006) at 45.22 km (Opportunity
  45.16 km); >90 % of distance driven using AutoNav (Spirit/Opportunity ~5 %); processes 600 000 points per look;
  up to 412 m per sol; mission extended to ~end 2028.
  https://science.nasa.gov/blogs/perseverance-mars-rover/2026/10/12/nasas-perseverance-sets-off-world-record-for-total-distance-driven/
  IEEE Spectrum: ~90 % autonomous by sol 1 312 (Oct 2024), Curiosity ~6.2 %.
- Perseverance AI-planned drives: 8 Dec 2025 (210 m) and 10 Dec 2025 (246 m); vision-language models chose waypoints
  from orbital (HiRISE) imagery and terrain data; checked in JPL digital twin, >500 000 telemetry variables, before
  uplink. https://astrobiology.com/2026/01/31/perseverance-rover-completes-first-ai-planned-drive-on-mars/ ;
  Nextgov 2026-10-20.
- Deep Space 1 Remote Agent Experiment (RAX), 17–21 May 1999: planner/scheduler + multithreaded Smart Executive +
  Livingstone model-based diagnosis; 2-day scenario from 11:04 PDT 17 May; 18 May deadlock (race condition, missing
  critical section in EXEC) stopped IPS-thrust termination; ~70 % of objectives met; 6-hour scenario 21 May; 100 %
  objectives; patch not uplinked. ~35 h total under RA control. Nayak et al., iSAIRAS 1999,
  https://ai.jpl.nasa.gov/public/documents/papers/rax-results-isairas99.pdf ; JPL news "Remote Agent experiment
  meets all objectives".
- EO-1 Autonomous Sciencecraft (ASE): onboard since Jan 2004, validated May 2004; runs on WARP Mongoose M5, 12 MHz
  (~8 MIPS), 256 MB; CASPER builds plans in tens of minutes; >11 000 autonomous acquisitions and >1 500 closed-loop
  re-tasks by 2007; ops cost reduction >$1M/yr; 2004–2017 primary operations, 60 000+ autonomous collects
  (Chien 2023 slides); >100× science events per MB downlinked.
  https://ai.jpl.nasa.gov/public/documents/papers/sherwood-smallsat07-eo1.pdf ;
  https://www.hpcuserforum.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Steve-Chien-Articial-Intelligence-in-Space-and-the-Hunt-for-Life-Beyond-Earth.pdf
- OSIRIS-REx TAG 20 Oct 2020 (Nightingale): Natural Feature Tracking delivered spacecraft within ~1 m of target;
  3.5 cm and 1.4 s from predicted touch. Norman et al. (Univ. Arizona).
- DART: SMART Nav (DRACO camera) autonomous terminal guidance for last ~4 h; Dimorphos resolved only ~1 h before
  impact (26 Sep 2022). https://www.nasa.gov/solar-system/smart-nav-giving-spacecraft-the-power-to-guide-themselves/
- Mars 2020 Lander Vision System (18 Feb 2021): HiRISE (~30 cm) maps resampled to 12 m (coarse) and 6 m (fine); coarse
  stage 15 landmarks over 3 images (128×128 templates, 1024×1024 search, FFT correlation); fine stage up to 150
  landmarks/image (15×15 templates in 100×100 windows); fused with IMU; position error requirement <40 m vs up to
  3.2 km inertial-only; touched down within 5 m of the LVS-selected point.
  https://robotics.jpl.nasa.gov/what-we-do/flight-projects/mars-2020-rover/terrain-relative-navigation ;
  https://science.nasa.gov/technology/technology-highlights/terrain-relative-navigation-landing-between-the-hazards
- SLIM (19 Jan 2024): all 14 image-matching navigation fixes accurate; 2 hazard detections, final target moved ~12 m;
  position accuracy before final hazard avoidance <10 m, possibly 3–4 m (JAXA PM Sakai); touchdown ~55 m from target
  after nozzle loss. https://global.jaxa.jp/press/2024/01/file/jaxa_doc01_20240125_e.pdf
- SPEC2019 (ESA ACT + Stanford SLAB, Feb–Jul 2019): SPEED ~15 000 synthetic (12 000 train, 2 998 test) + 305 real
  (5 train, 300 test) images of PRISMA Tango; 48 teams; winner UniAdelaide score 0.0094 synthetic (0.41° ± 1.50°,
  3.2 cm ± 9.5 cm) but 0.3752 on real; EPFL_cvlab best on real (0.1140). Score = angle error (rad) + position error /
  range. Kisantal et al. 2020 IEEE TAES, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.02050
- SPEC2021: SPEED+ 59 960 synthetic training images, 9 531 hardware-in-the-loop test images (lightbox 6 740, sunlamp
  2 791) from SLAB TRON; winners TangoUnchained (lightbox, ~0.07) and lava1302 (sunlamp, ~0.06); VPU 2nd in both.
  https://kelvins.esa.int/pose-estimation-2021/ ; Park et al. 2023 Acta Astronautica; arXiv 2303.13241 table.
- G-FOLD (Açıkmeşe, JPL): flight-tested on Masten Xombie 2012–13 (3-year, 7-flight campaign); July 2013 750 m divert
  at ~24–25 m/s; Bsocp solver ~100 ms on 1.4 GHz Pentium M. Masten/JPL press; arXiv 2106.09125.
- LeLaR: deep-RL attitude controller trained in simulation, flown on InnoCube 3U (launched 14 Jan 2025, Würzburg/TU
  Berlin); first full manoeuvre 30 Oct 2025; compared with classical PD. https://arxiv.org/html/2512.19576v1
- Astrobee ML warm-start: 13 Feb 2025 on ISS, neural network warm-starts sequential convex programming; −60 %
  iterations (rotational), −50 % (obstacles). Banerjee, Cauligi, Pavone, arXiv 2505.05588.
- Mars 2020 onboard planner ("Simple Planner"/OBP): development from 2016, V&V from Oct 2021, deployed to operations
  5 Oct 2023; priority-first, non-backtracking; event-triggered rescheduling; hundreds of plans run on Mars, very few
  anomalies. https://ai.jpl.nasa.gov/public/documents/presentations/OBP-ASTRA-2025.pdf
- AEGIS: Curiosity ChemCam from May 2016 (54 uses in first year, >93 % desired-target selection vs ~24 % blind);
  Perseverance SuperCam from 2022 (first 18 May 2022); totals 596 (MSL) and 293 (M2020) targets as of 11 May 2026.
  https://ai.jpl.nasa.gov/public/projects/aegis ; Francis et al. 2017 Science Robotics.
- Starling: 4 CubeSats (launched July 2023); Starling 1.5 coordination with Starlink; Starling 1.5+ (summer 2025)
  updated Distributed Spacecraft Autonomy, StarFOX star-tracker navigation tracking other objects, autoNGC
  collision-avoidance planning; extended to Dec 2025.
  https://nasa.gov/blogs/smallsatellites/2025/09/23/nasa-satellite-swarms-expanded-mission-powers-smarter-operations
- CADRE: three mini-rovers on Intuitive Machines IM-3, NET Q1 2027 (Reiner Gamma). Wikipedia IM-3; JPL.
- Proba-3: launched 5 Dec 2024 (PSLV-XL); ~150 m separation, millimetre-level relative position; first artificial
  eclipse image 23 May 2025; fully commissioned for autonomous formation flying June 2025; up to ~5–6 h eclipses.
  https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Images/2026/05/First_artificial_solar_eclipse_of_ESA_s_Proba-3_mission
- Canadarm3: Gateway paused (24 Mar 2026); MDA continues under CSA contract; refocused on lunar-surface logistics;
  launch NET 2029. https://payloadspace.com/mda-space-will-repurpose-canadarm3-for-lunar-surface/ ; Aviation Week.
- GITAI S2 arm on outside of Bishop airlock, March 2024, ISAM tasks, TRL 7 (Voyager). GITAI S1 inside Bishop 2021.
  GITAI USA among Golden Dome SBI OTA awardees (Apr 2026, currency_2026).
- Fast rendezvous: Tianzhou-5 docked 2 h 7 min after launch (12 Nov 2022), beating Soyuz MS-17 (3 h 3 min, Oct
  2020); Shenzhou-21 ~3.5 h (Oct/Nov 2025), Shenzhou-20 6.5 h. Wikipedia Tianzhou 5; AP via Arab News.
- ispace Hakuto-R M1 (25 Apr 2023): software misjudged altitude after crossing a crater rim and rejected good
  altimeter data — lander hovered ~5 km up until fuel ran out. Resilience (M2, 6 Jun 2025): laser rangefinder slow /
  degraded; telemetry lost 193 m up at 42 m/s. ispace 2025-06-24 press release; Payload; SpacePolicyOnline.
- IM-1 Odysseus (22 Feb 2024): laser rangefinder safety switch not flipped; patch to use NASA NDL; later account
  (Space Review) says a data flag prevented NDL data use; landed on its side.
- DeepMoon (Silburt et al. 2019 Icarus): U-Net on lunar DEM, recall 92 % on human catalogue, ~doubled crater
  detections, false-positive rate of new craters ~11 %.
- Hayabusa2 second touchdown 11 Jul 2019: 60 cm from aim point using target marker; autonomous final descent;
  descent start lowered 45→30 m. JAXA press 2019-07-25.
- Stargaze (SpaceX, announced 29–30 Jan 2026): ~30 000 Starlink star trackers, ~30 million transits/day, conjunction
  screening in minutes; free service. Spaceflight Now 2026-01-30; Starlink update.
- CNES autonomy: DEMETER autonomous orbit control experiment (from Apr 2005), PICARD autonomous GNC kit (Mar 2014),
  ASTERIA autonomous orbit control on ESA OPS-SAT (2021). OPS-SAT re-entered 22–23 May 2024. SmallSat 2022 paper.
