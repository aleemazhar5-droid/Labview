## Reanalysis
- ERA5: 1940–present, hourly, 0.25° (~31 km), 137 model levels; ERA6 now in production for C3S at ~14 km, first public streams (2007 onward) expected towards end of 2027. https://climate.copernicus.eu/copernicus-climates-era6-reanalysis-production-starts ; https://www.ecmwf.int/en/about/media-centre/science-blog/2026/reanalysis-past-present-future-weather-climate

## ML weather models
- FourCastNet (NVIDIA et al., arXiv Feb 2022; AFNO): 0.25°, week-long forecast in < 2 s; paper claims ~80 000× faster time-to-solution than NWP. https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.11214 ; https://docs.nvidia.com/physicsnemo/ (fcn_afno README)
- Pangu-Weather (Huawei; Nature Jul 2023): 3D Earth-Specific Transformer, 13 pressure levels, trained ERA5 1979–2017; hierarchical temporal aggregation (1, 3, 6, 24 h models). https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.02556
- GraphCast (Google DeepMind; Science Nov 2023): beat HRES on 90 % of 1 380 targets; 10-day forecast < 1 min on one TPU v4; training ~4 weeks on 32 TPU v4; ERA5 1979–2017; 0.25°, 37 levels, 6 h step. https://deepmind.google/blog/graphcast-ai-model-for-faster-and-more-accurate-global-weather-forecasting/ ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.12794
- FengWu (2023): skilful Z500 (ACC > 0.6) to 10.75 days. https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.02948
- FuXi (Fudan, npj Clim Atmos Sci 2023): cascade, 15-day, comparable to ECMWF ensemble mean at 15 days. https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.12873
- GenCast (Google DeepMind; Nature 4 Dec 2024): diffusion, 0.25°, 12 h steps, 15 days; beat ENS on 97.2 % of 1 320 targets (99.8 % beyond 36 h); 8 min per 15-day forecast on one TPU v5, members in parallel; trained to 2018, tested 2019. https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/gencast-predicts-weather-and-the-risks-of-extreme-conditions-with-sota-accuracy/ (arXiv v1 said 97.4 %)
- NeuralGCM (Google; Nature Jul 2024): hybrid differentiable dynamical core + learned physics, 2.8°/1.4°/0.7°. (Known paper; status = research, open code.)
- Aurora (Microsoft; Nature May 2025): 1.3 B parameters, > 1 million hours of data, 0.1° forecasts, ~5 000× faster than IFS (Microsoft claim), matches/beats GraphCast & IFS on 94 % of targets; air quality, waves, TC tracks. https://labs.ai.azure.com/innovations/aurora/ ; https://news.microsoft.com/source/features/ai/microsofts-aurora-ai-foundation-model-goes-beyond-weather-forecasting/
- Prithvi WxC (NASA/IBM 2024): 2.3 B, MERRA-2 (from Ch 44 table).
- WeatherNext 2 (Google DeepMind, Nov 2025): Functional Generative Network (~180 M parameters per seed vs 57 M GenCast); Google claims better than previous release on 99.9 % of variables/lead times; hundreds of scenarios in < 1 min on one TPU; used in Search, Pixel Weather, Maps Weather API. https://www.marktechpost.com/2025/11/17/google-deepminds-weathernext-2-uses-functional-generative-networks-for-8x-faster-probabilistic-weather-forecasts/
- WeatherNext 3 preprint (arXiv 2609.03582, 3 Sep 2026): ingests low-latency geostationary data, hourly steps, 0.1° single-level fields; combines DA, forecast, post-processing — research. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03582
- Google cyclone model (Weather Lab, launched 12 Jun 2025) used by NHC in 2025; NHC 2025 verification: GDMI slightly outperformed official Atlantic track forecasts 12–72 h; E. Pacific considerably better than other models 48–120 h. https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/weather-lab-cyclone-predictions-with-ai/ ; https://www.theweathernetwork.com/en/news/weather/severe/this-ai-weather-model-helped-forecasters-predict-2025s-huge-hurricanes (12 Apr 2026)
- Aardvark Weather (Nature, published Mar/Apr 2025; Cambridge, Turing, Microsoft, ECMWF): end-to-end from raw obs (ASCAT, AMSU-A/B, MHS, HIRS, IASI PCs, GridSat GEO IR, HadISD stations, ICOADS, IGRA radiosondes); gridded output 1.41°, 5 levels(?); forecast ~1 s on 4 A100 GPUs vs ~1 000 node-hours for HRES DA+forecast; beats GFS on many variables using ~10 % of inputs; station forecasts skilful to 10 days. https://arxiv.org/html/2404.00411 ; https://aihub.org/?p=17257
- GraphDOP (ECMWF, arXiv Dec 2024 + 2025 follow-ups): trained/initialised only from observations; skilful to ~5 days; research. https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15687
- NVIDIA Earth-2: open models announced 26 Jan 2026 at AMS (Atlas medium-range 15 days >70 variables; StormScope 0–6 h km-scale nowcasting from GEO satellite data over CONUS; HealDA data assimilation, release later in 2026); CorrDiff downscales 25 km → 2 km over Taiwan (CWA data). https://research.nvidia.com/labs/climate/post/2026.01.26-amslaunch-healda-atlas-stormscope ; https://docs.nvidia.com/physicsnemo/ (CorrDiff)

## Operational ML at national centres
- ECMWF AIFS Single operational 25 Feb 2025 (28 km, ~1 000× less energy per forecast, up to 20 % gains); AIFS ENS operational 1 Jul 2025 (06 UTC run; N320 ~31 km; 50+1 members; CRPS-trained); both upgraded to v2 on 12 May 2026 together with IFS Cycle 50r1 (wave component = first operational data-driven wave forecasts; snow variables). Experimental Aurora, FourCastNet, GraphCast, Pangu runs at ECMWF stopped at that change. https://www.ecmwf.int/node/29308 ; https://www.ecmwf.int/node/29407 ; https://confluence.ecmwf.int/display/FCST/Implementation+of+AIFS+Single+v2
- AIFS paper (arXiv 2406.01465): GNN encoder/decoder + sliding-window transformer processor; N320 ~31 km, processor O96 ~1°; 13 pressure levels; 6 h step; pre-train ERA5 1979–2020, fine-tune on operational analyses 2019–2020; ~1 week on 64 A100 GPUs; 10-day forecast ~2.5 min on one A100; area-weighted MSE, weights decreasing with height. https://arxiv.org/html/2406.01465
- NOAA (Dec 2025, Project EAGLE): AIGFS (16-day forecast in ~40 min, 0.3 % of GFS compute; weaker TC intensity), AIGEFS 31 members (9 % of GEFS compute), HGEFS 62-member hybrid (first operational hybrid physics-AI ensemble, per NOAA); GraphCast-based, fine-tuned with GDAS analyses. https://ecomagazine.com/news/research/noaa-deploys-new-generation-of-ai-driven-global-weather-models/ (18 Dec 2025)
- DWD AICON: Anemoi-based GNN, ~13 km grid (ICON-Global grid), up to 180 h; runs since 3 Sep 2025, data public / fully operational 2 Mar 2026. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.24651
- CMA Fengqing (with Tsinghua): operational, integrated into forecaster platform, V1.5; precipitation threat scores comparable to NWP within 10 days. https://typhooncommittee.org/20IWS/docs/Technical%20Presentations/4.%20FengQing%20and%20Its%20Performance%20in%202025%20Typhoon%20Season.pdf
- Met Office / Turing FastNet: research, not operational (Jul 2026), comparable RMSE to Global Model. https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/about-us/news-and-media/media-centre/weather-and-climate-news/2026/new-research-shows-ai-can-produce-trustworthy-physically-realistic-weather-forecasts-

## Limits
- Zhang, Fischer, Zscheischler, Engelke: HRES beats GraphCast, Pangu, FuXi on record-breaking heat/cold/wind (arXiv Aug 2025; Science Advances 2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.15724 ; KIT press release 040/2026
- Charlton-Perez et al. 2024 (npj): ML models captured Storm Ciarán track but underestimated peak winds (gust 111 kt Pointe du Raz). https://www.reading.ac.uk/news/2024/Research-News/AI-weather-forecasts-captured-Ciarans-destructive-path

## Nowcasting / observations
- DGMR (DeepMind + Met Office, Nature Sep 2021): 20 min of radar in, 90 min out; >50 meteorologists ranked first in 89 % of cases. https://deepmind.google/blog/article/nowcasting
- MetNet-3 (Google, 1 Nov 2023): up to 24 h, 2-min, 1–4 km; operational in CONUS + 27 European countries in Google products. https://research.google/blog/metnet-3-a-state-of-the-art-neural-weather-model-available-in-google-products/
- GREMLIN (CIRA): CNN making synthetic radar from GOES ABI+GLM; research, NWS briefing Jul 2025, not operational. https://www.cira.colostate.edu/ml/ml4da
- Dvorak: NHC post-analysis uncertainty 10–20 % when only satellite estimates exist (NASA study).

## Climate
- ACE2 (Ai2): ~1 500 simulated years/day on one GPU at 1°; trained on ERA5 1940–2020 (with held-out years). SamudrACE coupled ocean–atmosphere (arXiv 2509.12490). https://allenai.org/blog/ai2-climate-emulator
- Destination Earth phase 3 June 2026–June 2028 (ECMWF, ESA, EUMETSAT; Climate Change Adaptation DT + Weather-Induced Extremes DT; phase 3 to couple ML components into a European AI Earth-system model). https://www.ecmwf.int/en/about/media-centre/news/2026/destination-earth-moves-its-third-phase
- ESA CCI Open Data Portal: >100 satellite-derived climate datasets; satellites contribute to more than half of the 54 GCOS ECVs. https://www.esa.int/Applications/Observing_the_Earth/Space_for_our_climate/ESA_s_Climate_Change_Initiative

## Space weather
- DAGGER (FDL/NASA, 2023): global ground-magnetic perturbation 30 min ahead, < 1 s, updated every minute; tested on Aug 2011 and Mar 2015 storms; preprint notes ~persistence-level MAE/RMSE and underprediction near 1 000 nT. https://www.nasa.gov/feature/goddard/2023/sun/nasa-enabled-ai-predictions-may-give-time-to-prepare-for-solar-storms ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.12734
- Surya (NASA/IBM, 22 Aug 2025): ~366 M parameters, ~9 years SDO, ~218 TB; flare forecasts ~2 h ahead, ~16 % better than benchmarks (preliminary). https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14112
- Flare TSS: Bobra & Couvidat 2015 SVM on SHARP: TSS 0.76 (operational mode), precision ~42 %. Leka et al. 2019 operational comparison: most methods above no-skill, TSS roughly 0–0.5. https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.1405 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.02909
- CME arrival: Kay et al. 2024 scoreboard re-analysis: bias −2.5 h, MAE 13.2 h, SD 17.4 h; Riley et al. 2018 yearly MAE 11.2–22.6 h; CMETNet MAE 9.75 h (363 CMEs); CAT-PUMA 5.9 h (182 CMEs). https://predsci.com/~epalmerio/pubs/Kay2024b.pdf ; https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.02803
- Solar wind from AIA EUV: WindNet (Upendran et al. 2020) RMSE 82.3 km/s up to 4 days; Brown et al. 2022 71.9 km/s. https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.05825
- Kp ML: GFZ ML Kp ensemble forecast to 72 h, hourly (PAGER project; trained OMNI 1998–2019). https://spaceweather.gfz.de/products-data (via search snippet; site 503 on fetch). Lund (IRF) neural-network space-weather forecasting since ~1990s, ESA real-time prototype ~2001.
- AR 12192 (Oct 2014): 6 X, 22 M, 53 C flares; all X-class confined, no CME. https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.00473
- SOLAR-1 (ex-SWFO-L1): launched 24 Sep 2025 with IMAP; final burn at L1 23 Jan 2026, renamed SOLAR-1; operational service 2026 (exact date unconfirmed). SWPC planned an ML solar-wind classifier (Gaussian process) for SWFO-L1 data (2023 presentation) — status unconfirmed. https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/news/swfo-l1-renamed-solar-1-reaches-final-destination-one-million-miles-earth
- No confirmed operational ML flare/CME product at NOAA SWPC or Met Office MOSWOC (searches 26 Oct 2026); centres rely on physics models (WSA–Enlil, Geospace) plus human forecasters.
