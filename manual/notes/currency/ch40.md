# Chapter 40 — In-Space Servicing, Assembly and Manufacturing: currency notes (checked 19 Oct 2026)

All facts after 2023 used in the chapter, with sources. Date checked: 2026-10-19 unless stated.

## Life extension in GEO (Northrop Grumman SpaceLogistics)
- MEV-1: launched 9 Oct 2019 (Proton-M); docked with Intelsat 901 on 25 Feb 2020 in the graveyard orbit; IS-901 back in service 2 Apr 2020; MEV-1 undocked 9 Apr 2025 after IS-901 was returned to the graveyard orbit; MEV-1 then went on to Optus D3 (since separated; exact dates not confirmed -> chapter says "moved on to an Optus satellite").
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mission_Extension_Vehicle
- MEV-2: launched 15 Aug 2020 (Ariane 5); docked with Intelsat 10-02 in its operating slot 12 Apr 2021; five-year service, undocking planned ~2026 — no report of undocking found -> "planned to move on after about five years".
  https://www.satellitetoday.com/innovation/2021/04/12/northrop-grumman-successfully-docks-second-mission-extension-vehicle-with-operational-intelsat-satellite
- Price: Intelsat reportedly paid ~US$13 M per year (Spaceflight Now 2020, citing SEC filings; not official). Intelsat extended MEV services by >= 4 years (May 2024 agreement).
  https://spaceflightnow.com/2020/04/21/aging-intelsat-satellite-resumes-operations-after-docking-of-robotic-servicer/ ;
  https://satnews.com/2024/05/23/intelsats-mission-extension-vehicles-will-greatly-extend-life-of-additional-satellites/
- MRV + three MEPs launched 21 Jul 2026 21:15 UTC on Falcon 9 (SLC-40; booster B1069 final flight). MRV carries DARPA/NRL RSGS payload with two robotic arms; grapples launch-adapter interface; ~1 year to reach GEO; each MEP adds up to ~8 years (Northrop: "up to eight years" for ~2 000 kg satellites; other sources 6–8). Named clients include Optus and SES (SES absorbed Intelsat July 2025). After checkout RSGS goes to US Space Force.
  https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/07/21/live-coverage-spacex-to-launch-novel-geosynchronous-robotic-servicing-satellite-on-decade-long-mission/ ;
  https://mynews13.com/fl/orlando/space/2026/07/21/spacex-mrv-mep-mission ;
  https://defensescoop.com/2026/07/22/northrop-grumman-launches-mission-robotic-vehicle-jetpack-pods/

## NASA OSAM-1 / OSAM-2
- OSAM-1 (ex-Restore-L; refuel Landsat 7, SPIDER arm to assemble antenna & build beam) cancelled 1 Mar 2024: "continued technical, cost, and schedule challenges, and a broader community evolution away from refueling unprepared spacecraft". ~US$1.5 B appropriated; cost estimate grew from $626–753 M to >$2 B (OIG Oct 2023: up to $2.17 B; launch 2027).
  https://www.space.com/nasa-cancels-osam-1-satellite-servicing-mission ; https://www.aip.org/fyi/nasa-cancels-big-budget-robotic-servicing-demonstration
- OSAM-2 (Archinaut One, Redwire): concluded in 2023 before flight. https://www.nasa.gov/mission/on-orbit-servicing-assembly-and-manufacturing-2-osam-2

## Astroscale
- ELSA-d: launched 22 Mar 2021; magnetic capture demonstration 25 Aug 2021; Jan 2022 anomaly (4 of 8 thrusters lost), capture attempt cancelled; servicer de-orbited, mission closed Jan 2024.
  https://www.satellitetoday.com/technology/2022/01/27/astroscale-pauses-elsa-d-mission-after-detecting-spacecraft-anomaly ; https://astroscale.com/?p=7627
- ADRAS-J (launched Feb 2024 on Electron): fly-around at 50 m from H-IIA upper stage (GOSAT 2009 launch) June–July 2024 (aborted autonomously once); approached to 15 m on ~30 Nov 2024 (announced Dec 2024).
  https://www.theregister.com/2024/12/13/astroscale_15m_space_junk/
- ADRAS-J2: capture & de-orbit same stage (≈11 m × 4 m, ≈3 t); JAXA contract ≈¥12 B; Isar Aerospace Spectrum launch contract signed 1 Sep 2026, launch Japanese FY2027 (to Apr 2028) from Andøya.
  https://isaraerospace.com/press/isar-aerospace-signs-launch-contract-with-astroscale-japan ; https://space.com/astroscale-contract-adras-space-junk-removal
- ELSA-M (520 kg, capture a OneWeb satellite): Isar launch selected Mar 2026; schedule slipping (FY2028 or later per one source) -> "planned".
- APS-R (300 kg refueller for USSF Tetra-5, hydrazine, GEO): planned launch 2026; not confirmed launched -> "planned".
  https://www.airandspaceforces.com/space-force-satellite-refueling-demos/

## ClearSpace / ESA
- ClearSpace-1: target changed (Apr 2024) to PROBA-1 (95 kg, launched 2001); OHB-led; four-arm capture; ESA page: launch 2029 (other sources 2028). https://www.esa.int/Space_Safety/ClearSpace-1
- ASSIST (GMV/ESA): docking + refuelling interface, active servicer tool + passive client fixture; engineering model TRL 5/6 2026–27.
  https://www.gmv.com/en-es/docs/assists-brochure

## Starfish Space
- Otter Pup 2 launched Jun 2025 (Transporter-14); original docking partner (D-Orbit ION) withdrew; new target Gilmour ElaraSat (May 2026); no docking confirmed as of Sept–Oct 2026.
- First full Otter launched on Transporter-18 (~1 Oct 2026) for NASA SSPICY ($15 M; inspect defunct US satellites). Other contracts: Intelsat GEO life extension, SSC ($37.5 M), SDA de-orbit ($52.5 M).
  https://satellitetoday.com/sustainability/2026/09/25/starfish-spaces-debut-otter-vehicle-hitches-a-ride-on-spacexs-transporter-18-launch ; https://www.geekwire.com/2026/spacex-rideshare-starfish-space-first-otter/

## Katalyst / Swift
- NASA $30 M SBIR Phase III (Sept 2025). LINK (425 kg) launched 3 Jul 2026 on Pegasus XL (last Pegasus); lost attitude control 25 Jul (2 of 3 reaction wheels failed); came within 12–15 km of Swift; reboost abandoned 19–20 Aug 2026; LINK re-entered 25 Sep 2026. Swift (decayed from ~600 km to <400 km) expected to re-enter by end of 2026.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swift_boost_mission ; https://spacepolicyonline.com/news/no-go-for-katalysts-reboost-of-nasas-swift-observatory/

## In-space manufacturing
- ESA/Airbus metal 3D printer: launched on NG-20 (Jan 2024), installed in Columbus; first metal part announced 6 Sep 2024; stainless-steel wire + laser.
  https://www.esa.int/Newsroom/Press_Releases/ESA_3D_prints_first_metal_part_on_the_International_Space_Station
- Flawless Photonics ZBLAN on ISS (2024): >11 km of fibre drawn (University of Adelaide IPAS, Sept 2024; earlier reports 5 and 8 km).
  https://www.adelaide.edu.au/ipas/news/list/2024/09/25/space-fibres-touch-down-for-in-depth-analysis
- Redwire BFF: first knee meniscus bioprinted on ISS (July 2023, announced Sept 2023).
- Varda W-series: W-1 launched Jun 2023, landed Utah 21 Feb 2024 (ritonavir); W-2 landed Koonibba 28 Feb 2025; W-3 landed 14 May 2025; W-4 (Jun 2025, first Varda bus) — outcome unclear; W-5 launched 28 Nov 2025, landed 29/30 Jan 2026 (first all-C-PICA heat shield); W-6 launched 30 Mar 2026, landed 18/19 May 2026. Funding US$187 M round 2025.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varda_Space_Industries ; https://www.innovationplaces.sa.gov.au/news/varda-w5-capsule-return-kooniba-test-range/
- Space Forge ForgeStar-1: launched Jun 2025; generated plasma (~1 000 °C) in orbit Dec 2025, >100 times by Mar 2026; ForgeStar-2 to return material.
  https://www.spaceforge.com/missions/forgestar-1
- Caltech SSPD-1 (launched 3 Jan 2023): MAPLE beamed power in orbit and detected on Caltech roof (2023); mission ~1 year.
  https://www.eas.caltech.edu/news/space-solar-power-project-ends-first-in-space-mission-with-successes-and-lessons-1
