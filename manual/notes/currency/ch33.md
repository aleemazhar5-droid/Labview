## Polaris Dawn (first commercial EVA)
- EVA 12 Sep 2024 from Crew Dragon Resilience; Jared Isaacman and Sarah Gillis; whole cabin depressurised (no airlock);
  ~45 h prebreathe with cabin lowered to ~8.6 psi; suits at ~5 psi (34 kPa) on 12-ft (3.7 m) umbilicals, no backpack;
  HUD in visor, copper + indium-tin-oxide visor coating; "Skywalker" frame; orbit 190 × 737 km for EVA; total EVA
  (depress→repress) 1 h 46 min. https://spaceflightnow.com/2024/09/12/first-non-government-spacewalk-goes-off-without-a-hitch-for-polaris-dawn-crew/

## DCS / prebreathe (NASA OCHMO technical brief OCHMO-TB-037 Rev D, dated 5 Oct 2026)
- R = P_N2(tissue)/P_suit; 360-min half-time tissue; EMU at 4.3 psi historically R ≤ 1.65; NASA-STD-3001 V2 6008: < 4 %
  probability of DCS with 95 % confidence in ground validation (74 exposures with 0 DCS frequentist; 47 Bayesian).
  Protocols: 4-h in-suit prebreathe at 14.7 psia; campout at 10.2 psia/26.5 % O2 (time at 10.2 limited to 8 h 40 min);
  exercise (CEVIS) protocol; ISLE (40 min mask O2 + 20 min depress to 10.2 psia + in-suit light exercise).
  "NASA has never experienced a Type II event in spaceflight." Cabin limits: 30 % O2 flammability, ppN2 < 6.8 psi preferred.
  https://www.nasa.gov/sites/default/files/atoms/files/dcs_technical_brief_ochmo.pdf
- Gernhardt et al., EVA chapter (Principles of Clinical Medicine for Space Flight, 2013 PDF on nasa.gov): Shuttle protocols
  accepted at R = 1.65; staged 10.2 psi protocol (1 h O2, ≥12 h at 10.2 psia, 75 min in-suit; as short as 40 min after 36 h);
  ground DCS 23.7 % (mostly joint pain) but none in >140 Shuttle EVAs; Apollo suit 25.8 kPa, Apollo EVAs averaged 1 000 Btu/h
  (≈290 W) with peaks 2 200 Btu/h; EMU designed for up to 1 600 Btu/h; EVA work efficiency index 0.39–0.51 (ISS 4-h in-suit
  992 min total; ISS CEVIS 902 min; Shuttle staged 758 min); 0.95 L drink bag; 770 suited training events → 352 symptoms,
  47 % hands (fingernail delamination); 14 of 22 EVA astronauts reported EVA-related shoulder injury.
  https://nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/gernhardt-eva-ops-chp-5.4-2013.pdf
- 2024 NASA briefing: if average EVA metabolic rate < 1 000 Btu/h, a 6 h 30 min EVA is reasonable.
  https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20240009328

## EMU hardware
- Enhanced EMU: 29.6 kPa; ISS configuration ≈145 kg (319 lb); 8 h primary + 30 min secondary; 18 built, 11 functional (2017).
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extravehicular_Mobility_Unit
- Primary O2 ~850–900 psia (~6 MPa), ~1.2 lb; Secondary Oxygen Pack ~6 000 psia, ~30 min. NASA ICES papers
  https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20090013081/downloads/20090013081.pdf
- SAFER: 24 GN2 thrusters, ~1.4 kg N2, Δv ≈ 3.05 m/s, ~38 kg; first test STS-64, 16 Sep 1994.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Aid_For_EVA_Rescue
- EVA 23 (16 Jul 2013, Parmitano): 1.0–1.5 L water in helmet; reported 44 min in; EVA ended at 92 min; cause: silicate deposits
  clogging fan/pump/separator drum holes (contamination from ALCLR ion beds); prior EVA-22 water incident misdiagnosed.
  https://www.americaspace.com/2014/02/27/a-condition-that-was-life-threatening-nasa-releases-report-into-eva-23-water-intrusion-incident/
