# Chapter 50 — currency notes (checked late October 2026)

## SSA, conjunctions and space traffic
- ESA Kelvins Collision Avoidance Challenge (Oct–Dec 2019, two months): CDMs collected by ESA Space Debris Office
  2015–2019; database 15 321 events, 199 082 CDMs, 103 attributes, avg 13 CDMs/event (max 23); high-risk events
  r ≥ 1e-4: 30, ≥ 1e-5: 131, ≥ 1e-6: 515; 96 teams, 862 submissions; score = MSE(high-risk)/F2 with high risk
  log10 r ≥ −6; naive "latest risk" forecast very hard to beat; LightGBM gave ~1.3 % F2 gain over the LRP baseline
  in 505 Monte Carlo splits (0.607 vs 0.600). Uriot et al., Astrodynamics 6(2):121–140 (2022), arXiv 2008.03069.
- ESA (22 Oct 2019, "Automating collision avoidance"): a typical LEO satellite gets hundreds of alerts a week; for
  ESA's LEO fleet ~2 alerts per satellite per week need detailed analyst follow-up (hours each); manoeuvre if Pc >
  ~1e-4; ESA performs >1 avoidance manoeuvre per satellite per year. https://www.esa.int/Space_Safety/Space_Debris/Automating_collision_avoidance
- ESA CREAM (article 12 Aug 2025): started 2020; prototype by GMV and Guardtime, ground-based; provides alerts,
  actionable manoeuvres, coordination; entering expanded pilot phase; in-orbit demos ("piggyback" digital payload and a
  dedicated mission) being prepared. Modules AutoCA and AutoSTM (GMV paper, 9th European Conf. Space Debris, 2025).
  https://www.esa.int/Space_Safety/Space_Debris/CREAM_avoiding_collisions_in_space_through_automation
- TraCSS (FedScoop, 17 Jul 2026): still a pilot, 62 pilot users and >11 000 satellites; OSC FY2026 enacted
  $52.5 M; FY2027 request $11 M (does not mention TraCSS); OSC studying future models (contractor-owned, data as a
  service, fees). https://fedscoop.com/space-commerce-official-tracss-continues-despite-budget-uncertainty/
- EU SST: 2026 leaflet >700 satellites protected; >400 organisations; in 2023 detected 15 639 close approaches, ~1 000
  high risk (eusst.eu services page / news). https://www.eusst.eu/sites/default/files/documents/EUSST_Leaflet_2026.pdf
- Starlink manoeuvre statistics and Stargaze are covered in Chapter 48 (207 152 manoeuvres Dec 2025–May 2026;
  threshold 3e-7) — referenced, not repeated.
- US Space Force ATLAS (L3Harris) declared operationally accepted late Sept 2025 (Mission Delta 2), replacing 1990s
  SPADOC over time; no SPADOC shutdown date; JMS cancelled 2018. https://breakingdefense.com/2025/09/space-force-declares-atlas-space-domain-awareness-software-operational/
- USSF Data and AI FY2025 Strategic Action Plan (19 Mar 2025): Unified Data Library as program of record; SDA TAP Lab;
  LLM benchmarks for space ops. https://www.spaceforce.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4124351/
- LeoLabs (company AI page, 2025–26): ML for manoeuvre detection, patterns of life with "interest score", multi-object
  (break-up) detection, object characterisation; radar scheduling ML claims tracking objects "40% more frequently";
  Delta threat platform (2026). Vendor claims. https://leolabs.space/ai/
- Slingshot Aerospace "Agatha" (June 2024): DARPA-funded (PREDICT program from March 2023); trained on simulated
  constellation data; inverse reinforcement learning to flag outlier satellites in constellations and infer intent;
  results checked with operators. https://defensescoop.com/2024/06/05/darpa-agatha-harnesses-ai-keep-tabs-space-weapons-spy-satellites/
- Parker & Linares, "Satellite drag analysis during the May 2024 Gannon geomagnetic storm" (J. Spacecraft & Rockets;
  arXiv 2406.08617): ap peaked 400; NRLMSISE-00 density at 400 km up to 6× baseline; ap forecasts under-predicted by
  100–300; ~300 of ~10 000 active LEO payloads manoeuvring before vs "thousands" (nearly half) after, mostly
  Starlink; KANOPUS-V 3 decay 38 → 180 m/day. https://arxiv.org/html/2406.08617v2
- Licata, Mehta et al. HASDM-ML (Space Weather 2022; arXiv 2109.07651): ML surrogate of 20 years of SET HASDM density
  database, PCA + NN, ~10 % error vs HASDM with calibrated uncertainty (MC dropout).
- Peng & Bai, JSR 55(5) 2018: ANN learns orbit-prediction errors from historical OD/prediction data (also SVM, GP).
- Light curves: MMT (Mini-MegaTORTORA) data; CNN accuracy ~75 % (3 classes) to ~90 % (8 classes); RoBo6 benchmark
  (arXiv 2412.00544, Dec 2024); CoAtNet+EMD 92 % (IAC 2023). Different class sets — not comparable.
- StreakMind (A&A 2026, arXiv 2605.03429): YOLO oriented boxes on 2 335 images, precision 94 %, recall 97 %.
- Re-entry ML: Salmaso, Trisolini, Colombo, Aerospace 10(3) 297 (2023): seq2seq on TLE altitude profiles of >400 bodies.
- Victus Haze: Rocket Lab Electron launched Puma 19 Jun 2026, 16 h 42 min after notice; RPO with True Anomaly Jackal.
- Shijian-21/Shijian-25 apparent GEO docking (refuelling test) ~Jul 2025, separated ~Nov 2025; watched by COMSPOC,
  S2a, Slingshot, reportedly GSSAP; China did not confirm.

- NGA Luno A ($290 M, 10 vendors, Sept 2024) and Luno B (~$200 M, 13 vendors, Jan 2025) commercial analytics IDIQs.
- NRO: Sentient — declassified (2019 FOIA) description as automated tipping and cueing R&D; 2025 HASC statement on fusing
  data for automated tipping and cueing; proliferated architecture 14 launches May 2024–June 2026 (NROL-179),

- Golden Dome: EO 14186 (27 Jan 2025); reconciliation P.L. 119-21 $24.4 B for IAMD; Guetlein $185 B through 2035;
  CBO (12 May 2026) ~$1.2 T over 20 years (illustrative architecture), space-based interceptors ~70 % of acquisition;
  C2 "glue layer" consortium (9 firms incl. Lockheed, Northrop, RTX) live demo before Mar 2026, IOC 2028; JTF-Gold
  15 Jan 2026; first operational site Fort Story (Aug 2026, CRS); FY2027 request $17.5 B; 12 SBI companies Apr 2026
  ($3.2 B). Guetlein 14 Sep 2026: decisions "at machine speed", AI to help operators (Aviation Week).
  https://www.airandspaceforces.com/industry-consortium-live-c2-demo-golden-dome ;
  https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/2026-08-18_IF13264_98b6d5bc14b6abc45210ab374912f590c984fb77.html ;
  https://federalnewsnetwork.com/defense-news/2026/05/cbo-estimates-golden-dome-could-cost-1-2-trillion-over-20-years/

  from YOLO in aerial imagery (digital test only).

- SatIQ (Smailes et al., ACM CCS 2023): Iridium transmitter fingerprinting, ~1.7 M messages at 25 MS/s, EER 0.120
