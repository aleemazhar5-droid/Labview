Onboard and tasking
- JPL Dynamic Targeting on CogniSAT-6, mid-July 2025: satellite tilted 40–50° to look ~500 km ahead, analysed the image on board and decided to image or skip in < 90 s, no human in the loop; first flight test; next: fires, eruptions, storms. JPL news "How NASA is testing AI to make Earth-observing satellites smarter" https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/how-nasa-is-testing-ai-to-make-earth-observing-satellites-smarter/ ; concept paper https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.05304
- OroraTech: FOREST nanosats carry NVIDIA Jetson Xavier NX (up to 21 TOPS at 15 W); fire detection and compression on board, alerts in minutes (company/NVIDIA material; FOREST-1 launched Jan 2022). https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/ororatech-wildfires-from-space ; https://itu.int/en/ITU-T/webinars/20230419/Documents/4_AndreaSpichtinger.pdf
- FireSat (Earth Fire Alliance, Muon Space, Google): protoflight launched 14 Mar 2025 (Transporter-13); first three operational satellites launched on Transporter-17 (announced 7 Jul 2026), Condor-M bus, six-channel IR, fires as small as 5 m × 5 m, at least twice-daily revisit of fire-prone regions; goal hourly global revisit by 2029; first-light active-fire images released Sep 2026. https://www.muonspace.com/muon-space-deploys-first-three-operational-firesat-satellites-for-earth-fire-alliance/
- Tip-and-cue: Vantor (ex-Maxar) Maritime Sentry combines EO, SAR, RF, AIS with AIS-to-EO and SAR-to-EO tipping for dark vessels; machine-to-machine cueing between commercial operators still largely research (Synspective blog 2025; Technion arXiv 2512.09670). https://developers.maxar.com/sentry/maritime/product-overview ; https://synspective.com/blogs/2025/kumar_blog2/ ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.09670
- Satellogic: Palantir Edge AI ran on a NewSat launched Apr 2022 (Transporter-4). https://blog.palantir.com/edge-ai-in-space-93d793433a1e

Land
- ESA WorldCover: 10 m, 11 classes, from Sentinel-1 and -2; overall accuracy 74.4 % (2020 v100) and 76.7 % ± 0.5 % (2021 v200, released 28 Oct 2022); different algorithms in the two years so differences are not all real change. https://worldcover2021.esa.int ; https://pure.iiasa.ac.at/18981/1/WorldCover_PVR_V2.0.pdf
- Dynamic World (Brown et al., Sci. Data 9:251, June 2022): 9 classes, 10 m, per-pixel probabilities, every Sentinel-2 L1C image with ≤ 35 % cloud since 27 Jun 2015; trained on ~24 000 tiles of 5.1 km × 5.1 km labelled by 25 experts + crowd labellers; 73.8 % agreement with a 409-tile expert-consensus test set (7.5 % above next best global product). https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/GOOGLE_DYNAMICWORLD_V1 ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9184477/table/Tab10
- Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints: ~1.4 billion buildings from Bing Maps imagery 2014–2024 (+ Vexcel 2021–25); per-region precision 92–97 %, recall 71–86 % (Africa 94.4 / 70.9 %, Europe 94.3 / 85.9 %); last refresh 13 Aug 2026. https://github.com/microsoft/GlobalMLBuildingFootprints
- Google Open Buildings v3: 1.8 billion building detections over 58 million km² in Africa, South and South-East Asia, Latin America and the Caribbean. https://source.coop/vida/google-microsoft-open-buildings
- Global Forest Watch (UMD GLAD data, WRI release 29 Apr 2026): tropical primary forest loss 4.3 Mha in 2025, down 36 % from record 6.7 Mha in 2024; fires 42 % of 25.5 Mha total tree-cover loss in 2025. https://www.wri.org/news/release-tropical-rainforest-loss-drops-36-2025-fires-threaten-global-progress
- RADD alerts (Wageningen; Reiche et al. 2021 ERL): Sentinel-1 C-band, 10 m, every 6–12 days, ~50 tropical countries, alert from a single new image; confirmed (high confidence) if disturbance probability > 97.5 % within 90 days, low-confidence > 85 %; MMU 0.1 ha. https://www.wur.nl/en/research/products-services/radd-forest-disturbance-alert ; https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1748-9326/abd0a8
- ESA WorldCereal (ESSD Dec 2023): first global 10 m seasonal crop extent, maize, cereals and irrigation maps for 2021 (released 2023); temporary-crop extent user's/producer's accuracy 88.5 % / 92.1 %; crop type ≥ 80 % agreement with in-situ data. https://pure.iiasa.ac.at/18979
- PSE+TAE (Garnot et al., CVPR 2020) on S2-Agri: OA 94.2 %, mIoU 50.9 %. https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.07757
- Amazon Mining Watch (Earth Genome, Pulitzer Center): CNN ensemble on Sentinel-2, nine Amazon countries, yearly 2018–2024, quarterly from Q2 2025; Aug 2026 update ~99 % specificity, 90 % sensitivity. https://www.earthgenome.org/blog/amazon-mining-watch-august-2026-update

Oceans and maritime
- Paolo et al., Nature 625, 85–91 (3 Jan 2024): ~2 PB imagery 2017–2021; 753 030 Sentinel-1 scenes; 2 494 370 Sentinel-2 scenes; 53 billion AIS messages; CFAR + three CNNs; vessel presence ~97.5 % accuracy, length R² 0.84; fishing/non-fishing 90.5 %; 23.1 million detections; ~63 300 vessels at sea at any time; 72–76 % of industrial fishing vessels and 21–30 % of transport/energy vessels not publicly tracked; ~28 000 offshore structures; detection > 70 % at 25 m, > 90 % ≥ 50 m. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10764273
- xView3-SAR (Paolo et al., NeurIPS 2022 D&B): close to 1 000 Sentinel-1 scenes, ~29 400 × 24 400 px each, with bathymetry and wind rasters. https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.00897
- AutoICE (ESA AI4EO, 2023): Sentinel-1 + AMSR2 → sea-ice concentration, stage of development, floe size; winning multi-task U-Net combined score 86.4 %. https://tc.copernicus.org/articles/18/1621/2024/

Disasters
- Sentinel-1A ended operations 30 Jun 2026 after 12 years; Sentinel-1C and -1D now the operational pair; 1D data open 17 Apr 2026. https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/node/4026
- Copernicus GFM: flood masks for every new Sentinel-1 IW image; ensemble of three algorithms (LIST, DLR, TU Wien), majority vote, mean likelihood; integrated in GloFAS 2021; v4.1.1 (11 Jun 2026) added Sentinel-1D. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.12488 ; https://global-flood.emergency.copernicus.eu/news/246-gfm-version-411-welcomes-sentinel-1d/
- Sen1Floods11 (Bonafilia et al., CVPRW 2020): 4 831 chips 512 × 512, 120 406 km², 11 flood events, 6 continents; 446 hand-labelled chips; models trained on many weak (optical-derived) labels beat models trained on few hand labels. https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPRW_2020/papers/w11/Bonafilia_Sen1Floods11_A_Georeferenced_Dataset_to_Train_and_Test_Deep_Learning_CVPRW_2020_paper.pdf
- International Charter: activation 1052 (Nepal flash flood, 26 Aug 2026) → > 1 050 activations since 2000; 85 activations in 2024, a record (Germany statement, COPUOS STSC 2025). https://sentinel-asia.org/EO/2026/article20260826NP.html ; https://www.unoosa.org/documents/pdf/copuos/stsc/2025/Statements/6_Germany.pdf
- Copernicus EMS rapid mapping: 572 activations 2012–Apr 2022; Myanmar earthquake Mar 2025: 93 maps from 106 post-event images. https://emergency.copernicus.eu/mapping/list-of-activations-rapid
- Microsoft AI for Good + Planet + AFAD, Turkey Feb 2023: xView2-based model, imagery from first 3 days, ~3 849 buildings damaged/destroyed in four cities; Kahramanmaraş 7.44 %. Direct Relief (Dec 2025): Microsoft and Google models agreed in places but disagreed significantly elsewhere. https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/turkey-earthquake-report/ ; https://www.directrelief.org/2025/12/after-disaster-ai-models-support-rapid-understanding-of-health-facility-damage/
- SKAI (WFP + Google Research): open-source damage assessment; UNOSAT evaluation over nine events: ~7× larger area, ~6× faster, under a day; few damaged examples hurts accuracy. https://www.unglobalpulse.org/ai-from-google-research-and-un-boosts-humanitarian-disaster-response-wider-coverage-faster-damage-assessments

Greenhouse gases
- UNEP IMEO MARS: launched COP27 (Nov 2022); Oct 2025 "Eye on Methane" report: > 3 500 alerts, 33 countries, response rate rose from 1 % to 12 %; July 2026 release: > 5 000 alerts, 33 countries, 13 % response, > 40 verified mitigations in 10 countries. https://www.eco-business.com/press-releases/unep-partners-ramp-up-efforts-to-address-major-methane-leaks-as-un-chief-urges-rapid-cuts/ ; https://www.unep.org/news-and-stories/press-release/better-data-driving-action-methane-emissions-more-work-needed
- Carbon Mapper Tanager-1 first year (19 Sep 2024 – 16 Sep 2025): ~5 392 published methane plumes (oil & gas 2 661, waste 1 327, coal 1 211), 1 234 CO₂ plumes. https://carbonmapper.org/articles/tanager-1-one-year-in-space
- Schuit et al. 2023 (ACP 23, 9071): CNN + SVC on TROPOMI 2021 → 2 974 plumes, mean 44 t/h (8–122 t/h 5–95 %), 94 persistent clusters. https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/23/9071/2023/
- MAPL-EMIT (Google + JPL, PNAS, 9 Sep 2026): trained on 3.6 million physics-simulated plumes; detects ~50 % more plumes than human experts; > 23 000 additional plumes globally; database on Earth Engine. https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-research/mapping-global-methane-emissions-from-space/
- GHGSat: ~14 high-resolution (~25 m) satellites late 2025 (EGU26 abstract); three more launched July 2026 → "about 15–17"; use "more than a dozen". https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU26/EGU26-14349.html
- IME method: Q = U_eff·IME/L, L = √A; Sentinel-2 calibration U_eff = 0.33 U10 + 0.45 m/s (Varon et al. 2021, via NASA AGU 2022 poster). https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20220018115/downloads/2022AGU_Howell_SY15C-0426_v2.pdf

Development
- Chi et al., PNAS 119(3) e2113658119 (Jan 2022): relative wealth for 135 LMICs at 2.4 km; trained on DHS from 56 countries; explains 56–70 % of variation (CV); used by Togo/GiveDirectly for cash-transfer targeting. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8784134
