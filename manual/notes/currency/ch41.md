## Prospecting missions
- PRIME-1 (TRIDENT drill + MSolo) on IM-2 Athena: launched 27 Feb 2025 00:16 UTC, landed 6 Mar 2025 17:28 UTC at
  84.79° S, ~500 m from Mons Mouton target, on its side in a crater; ~12 h of operations vs ~10 days planned.
  TRIDENT demonstrated its range of motion but did not drill into the surface; MSolo powered on 3 times,
  43 scans; saw lander CH4/O2/He tank venting; sensitivity lower than in transit.
  Source: NASA NTRS "PRIME-1: MSolo Overview and Results" (Apr 2026)
  https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20260003150/downloads/MSolo%20PRIME-1%20overview%20and%20results%20-%20LSSW.pdf ;
  https://spacepolicyonline.com/news/im-2-mission-ends-one-day-after-lunar-landing/
- Lunar Trailblazer (SIMPLEx, ~$94M; HVM³ + LTM): launched 26 Feb 2025 on IM-2 Falcon 9; contact lost next day;
  mission ended 31 Jul 2025 (announced Aug 2025); likely power failure from misaligned arrays.
  https://science.nasa.gov/mission/lunar-trailblazer ; https://www.space.com/space-exploration/missions/nasa-declares-troubled-lunar-trailblazer-moon-orbiter-dead/
- ESA PROSPECT (ProSEED drill to ~1 m + ProSPA lab): now flies on Intuitive Machines IM-4 (NASA CLPS), 2027;
  originally on Russian Luna-27. https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Videos/2026/06/Prospect_mission ;
  https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Images/2026/10/Drilling_down_for_the_Moon
- LUPEX / Chandrayaan-5 (ISRO lander, JAXA rover, H3): approved by India Mar 2025; implementing arrangement
  29 Aug 2025; launch ~2028 (sources vary 2027–29); drill ~1 m. https://vajiramandravi.com/current-affairs/chandrayaan-5-mission/
- VIPER: CLPS CS-7 task order to Blue Origin, up to $190M (Sept 2025), late 2027 on 2nd Blue Moon MK1; option
  depends on first MK1 flight. https://interestingengineering.com/space/blue-origin-nasa-viper-rover-contract
- Chang'e-8 (~2028): ISRU test incl. solar-concentrator melting/3D printing of regolith bricks (1 400–1 500 °C);
  simulant bricks exposed on Tiangong. https://www.space.com/china-moon-3d-printing-bricks-change-8-2028
- Chandrayaan-3 ChaSTE (Communications Earth & Environment, Mar 2025): 82 °C on 6° Sun-facing slope, ~60 °C flat;
  pole-facing slopes >14° at high latitude may allow near-surface ice. https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/03/06/lst3-research-chandrayaan-3-ice.html

## Lunar ice science
- ShadowCam (KPLO) — Li et al., Science Advances, 18 Mar 2026: no evidence of widespread surface ice above
  ~20–30 wt% in PSRs; a few 20–50 m patches consistent with >10 % ice. https://earthsky.org/space/ice-in-moons-shadows-shadowcam-moon/
- LCROSS 5.6 ± 2.9 wt% water in Cabeus ejecta (Colaprete et al. 2010, Science) — pre-2024, standard.
- Cold traps ~40 000 km², ~60 % in south (Hayne et al. 2021, Nature Astronomy) — pre-2024.
- (Not used: a secondary source quoted "~100 Mt" for 10 m of ice over 10 000 km², which is inconsistent — that volume
  would be ~9 × 10¹⁰ t. Chapter instead gives its own illustrative estimate: 40 000 km² × 1 m × 1 500 kg/m³ × 5.6 % ≈ 3 Gt.)
- Chang'e-5 regolith heated >1 200 K yields 51–76 mg water per g (hydrogen in ilmenite), journal The Innovation,
  2024. https://interestingengineering.com/science/china-extracts-water-from-lunar-soil
