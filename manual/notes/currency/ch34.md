# Chapter 34 — Launch Vehicle Engineering: currency notes (checked 18 Oct 2026)

Falcon 9 vehicle and payload environment (SpaceX Falcon User's Guide, 9 May 2025 edition)
- Source: https://www.spacex.com/assets/media/falcon-users-guide-2025-05-09.pdf (downloaded and read 18 Oct 2026).
- Stage 1: Al-Li skin, aluminium domes, common dome between LOX and RP-1 tanks, friction-stir welded; LOX tank monocoque, fuel tank skin-and-stringer; 9 Merlin 1D, 845 kN each SL (7 605–7 686 kN total); throttle 190 000 → 108 300 lbf per engine; heated-helium pressurisation; TVC hydraulics use RP-1 as working fluid; four grid fins, four legs; each engine in its own metal bay; hold-down after ignition with autonomous safe shutdown.
- Stage 2: one MVac, 981 kN, fixed 165:1 nozzle, dual-redundant TEA-TEB igniters, GN2 attitude control; interstage carbon-fibre/aluminium-honeycomb; pneumatic stage-separation pushers (incl. redundant centre pusher); fairing 5.2 m dia, 13.2 m tall (extended 18.7 m), pneumatic latches/pushers (extended fairing uses frangible joint).
- Avionics: three-string fault-tolerant, single-fault tolerant. Aerospace Corp. (Chang 2001): 91 % of launch failures in previous two decades from engines, avionics, stage separation.
- Reuse (guide text): >430 Falcon launches by end 2024; first stages re-flown >384 times and fairing halves re-flown on 307 missions, both "as of February 2025", 100 % success.
- Quasi-static limit load factors, payloads >1 800 kg: axial +6.0 g (with ±0.5 g lateral) / +3.5 g with ±2.0 g lateral, tension −1.5 to −2.0 g; 1 000–1 800 kg: up to +8.5 g axial, ±3 g lateral; <1 000 kg: up to +11 g axial, ±7.5 g lateral. Recommended payload fundamental frequencies: lateral >10 Hz, axial >25 Hz, secondary structure >35 Hz.
- Acoustic MPE (P95/50, 60 % fill, standard fairing): with blankets OASPL 131.4 dB (1/3-octave) — peak ~122 dB at 160 Hz; without blankets 137.9 dB, peak 129.8 dB at 200 Hz; Falcon Heavy with blankets 135.2 dB.
- Shock at separation plane: fairing-sep SRS 30 g at 100 Hz → 300 g from 500 Hz (std fairing); clamp-band separation 30 g at 100 Hz → 1 000 g at 1 000–10 000 Hz.
- Random vibration MPE at top of PAF: 0.0044 g²/Hz 20–100 Hz, 0.01 g²/Hz 300–700 Hz, 0.03 g²/Hz 800–925 Hz, 0.00644 g²/Hz at 2 000 Hz; 5.13 grms.
- Fairing jettison when free-molecular heating < 1 135 W/m². Payload processing ISO Class 8 (Class 100 000).
- Test factors: qualification = limit × 1.25 (static, sine), acoustic/random MPE + 3 dB (2 min qual, 1 min accept). Design factors: yield 1.10, ultimate (flight) 1.25, ultimate (ground) 1.40.
- Sample timelines: GTO — max-Q T+74 s, MECO T+147 s, sep T+151 s, SES-1 T+158 s, fairing T+222 s, SECO-1 T+484 s, SES-2 T+1 636 s, SECO-2 T+1 696 s, payload sep T+1 996 s. LEO — max-Q T+67 s, MECO T+145 s, sep T+148 s, SES-1 156 s, fairing 195 s, SECO-1 514 s, SES-2 3 086 s, payload sep 3 390 s.
- GTO perigee baseline 185 km; LEO inclinations ≤55° from Eastern Range, 55–65° with dog-leg; SSO from either range; GTO only from Eastern Range.

Failures and investigations
- Starlink 9-3 (11/12 Jul 2024, Vandenberg): LOX leak from a crack in a sense line for a pressure sensor on the second-stage oxygen system (fatigue from engine vibration + loose clamp); cold leak chilled ignition-fluid components, MVac hard start on relight, stage lost attitude control, 20 Starlinks re-entered. FAA approved return to flight 25 Jul 2024; fix: remove line/sensor, inspect booster clamps. https://www.space.com/spacex-finds-cause-falcon-9-rocket-failure ; https://www.americaspace.com/2024/07/27/falcon-9-rapidly-returns-to-flight/
- Crew-9 (28 Sep 2024): off-nominal second-stage deorbit burn, debris outside hazard area; brief grounding. https://spaceflightnow.com/2024/09/29/spacex-grounds-its-falcon-rocket-fleet-after-upper-stage-misfire/
- Early 2026 Starlink 17-32: second-stage deorbit burn failed to ignite (gas bubble in transfer tube); brief stand-down, return to flight ~7 Feb 2026. https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/02/07/live-coverage-spacex-to-launch-return-to-flight-falcon-9-mission-following-brief-stand-down/
